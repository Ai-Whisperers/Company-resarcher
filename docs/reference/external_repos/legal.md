## 📊 Key Information

### Formation Timeline
| Phase | Duration | Cost Estimate |
|-------|----------|---------------|
| Pre-formation | 1-2 weeks | $0 |
| Notarization | 1-3 days | $100-150 |
| Public Registry | 1 week | $50-100 |
| Tax Registration (RUC) | 2-5 days | $0 |
| Banking Setup | 1 week | $50-100 |
| Invoicing Authorization | 1-2 days | $0-50 |
| Municipal Registration | 3-5 days | $40-100 |

### Tax Structure
- **Corporate Income Tax (IRACIS):** 10%
- **VAT (IVA):** 10% (5% for some export services)
- **Personal Income Tax on dividends (IRP):** Progressive to 10%

### Compliance Calendar
- **Monthly:** VAT declarations (due 15th of following month)
- **Annual:** IRACIS filing (March), financial statements, partners meeting, timbrado renewal

## 🔍 Key Advantages

1. **Service-Optimized** - E.A.S. specifically designed for professional services
2. **Cost-Effective** - Lower formation and maintenance costs
3. **Equal Partnership** - Perfect for 50/50 ownership
4. **International Ready** - Can serve global clients
5. **Limited Liability** - Partners protected from company debts
6. **Tax Efficient** - 10% corporate tax rate
7. **Simplified Compliance** - Less bureaucracy than S.R.L./S.A.
