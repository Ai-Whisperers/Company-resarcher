# Research Data Master List

**Purpose**: Complete taxonomy of ALL data points we want to research about target companies, organized by category.

This master list combines data from both **Investment Analysis** and **B2B Sales** strategies.

---

## 💰 Financial Health

- Total Revenue (YoY, QoQ)
- Revenue by Product Line
- Revenue by Geography
- Revenue by Currency
- Recurring vs. One-Time Revenue
- Customer Concentration (Top 3, Top 10)
- Gross Margin
- Operating Margin
- Net Margin
- EBITDA
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- Lifetime Value (LTV)
- LTV/CAC Ratio
- Payback Period
- Operating Cash Flow
- Free Cash Flow
- Burn Rate
- Runway (Months)
- Debt (Short-term, Long-term)
- Equity Raised (Total, Last Round)
- Valuation History
- Cap Table Concentration

## 🌍 Market Position

- TAM (Total Addressable Market)
- SAM (Serviceable Available Market)
- SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)
- Current Market Share (%)
- Market Share Trend (3Y)
- Share of Voice (Media mentions vs. competitors)
- Direct Competitors (Top 5)
- Indirect Competitors
- Competitive Advantages (Moat)
- Threat of New Entrants
- Revenue Growth Rate (YoY)
- User/Customer Growth Rate
- Market Growth Rate
- Growth vs. Market Average
- Current Regulations Impacting Business
- Pending Legislation
- Compliance Status (GDPR, CCPA, etc.)
- Regulatory Tailwinds/Headwinds

## 🔴 B2B Sales Signals (Social Media Intelligence)

- Post Frequency (Daily, Weekly, Monthly)
- Comment Volume (>50 comments/post)
- Unanswered Questions (Visible in comments)
- Response Time (>24 hours or no response)
- Negative Sentiment Spikes (Crisis events)
- Public Complaints (Twitter, Facebook, Instagram)
- Review Site Activity (Yelp, G2, Trustpilot)
- Complaint Resolution Time (Slow or none)
- Escalation Patterns (Same issues repeated)
- Employee Surveys (Annual, quarterly)
- Customer Questionnaires (NPS, CSAT)
- Feedback Forms (Website, app)
- Quality Audits (Call center, support tickets)
- PR Crises (Viral negative content)
- Competitor Sentiment Gap (They're doing better)
- Influencer Backlash
- Lost Customers (Mentioned in comments)
- Recent Fundraising (Fresh capital)
- Marketing Budget Announcements
- Customer Success Team Expansion
- Tech Stack Investments (CRM, analytics tools)
- CMO/Head of Customer Success LinkedIn Activity
- Conference Attendance (CX, Marketing events)
- Podcast Appearances (Talking about customer feedback)
- Job Posts (Customer Insights, Social Media Manager)
- Fiscal Year Start (New budgets)
- New CMO/CCO (New priorities)
- Product Launch (Need feedback monitoring)
- Negative PR Event (Urgency to fix reputation)
- Recent SaaS Purchases (Hootsuite, Sprout Social)
- CRM Implementation (Salesforce, HubSpot)
- Analytics Tools (Google Analytics, Mixpanel)
- API Integrations (Tech-savvy, open to new tools)
