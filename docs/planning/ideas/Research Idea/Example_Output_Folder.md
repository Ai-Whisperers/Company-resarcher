# Executive Summary

**Company:** Personal Paraguay

## One-Page Strategic Overview

Personal Paraguay is the second-largest mobile network operator in Paraguay with approximately 31% market share. As a subsidiary of Telecom Argentina (67.5% ownership), the company has evolved from a pure mobile provider into a multi-service telecommunications company offering mobile, internet, TV, and financial services.

|----------|----------|
| Customer-Centric Digital Strategy | "Keywords para la gente" campaign: +29% e-commerce sales, -35% cost per sale |
