National Telecommunications Commission (CONATEL) Paraguay

National Telecommunications Commission (CONATEL) in Paraguay plays a pivotal role in regulating and overseeing the telecommunications industry. As the governing body, CONATEL ensures that the telecom sector operates fairly and efficiently. They handle licensing, compliance, and spectrum management, among other responsibilities, to maintain a competitive and innovative telecommunications landscape in Paraguay. The National Telecommunications Commission, known as CONATEL (Comisión Nacional de Telecomunicaciones) in Paraguay, is a vital regulatory authority responsible for overseeing the nation's telecommunications and broadcasting sectors. CONATEL plays a pivotal role in shaping the telecommunications landscape in Paraguay.


About CONATEL


CONATEL Paraguay is an autonomous government entity that reports to the Office of the President of the Republic. Established under the Telecommunications Law (Law 642/95), CONATEL holds the authority to regulate, control, and supervise the telecommunications and broadcasting services in Paraguay.



Regulatory Functions


CONATEL's core functions encompass regulating the telecommunications and broadcasting sectors. They ensure that these services are available, accessible, and affordable to all Paraguayans. Furthermore, CONATEL promotes fair competition, safeguarding consumers' rights and fostering industry innovation.



Licensing and Spectrum Management


One of CONATEL's primary responsibilities is the allocation and management of frequency spectrum, a critical resource for radio and television broadcasting, mobile networks, and more. They issue licenses and ensure efficient use of these resources.



Consumer Protection


CONATEL places consumers at the forefront of its mission. They actively enforce regulations to protect the rights and interests of telecom and broadcasting service users. This includes quality of service, billing, and dispute resolution.



Promoting Innovation


The commission promotes technological innovation and investment in the sector, contributing to Paraguay's digital development. They create a conducive environment for telecom and broadcasting companies to thrive.



Digital Inclusion


CONATEL actively works towards reducing the digital divide in Paraguay. They aim to bring telecommunications and broadcasting services to even the most remote areas, ensuring connectivity for all Paraguayans.



Contact CONATEL


If you're a stakeholder in Paraguay's telecommunications or broadcasting sector, CONATEL is your go-to partner. Contact them for licensing, regulatory compliance, and any inquiries related to telecom and broadcasting in Paraguay.
