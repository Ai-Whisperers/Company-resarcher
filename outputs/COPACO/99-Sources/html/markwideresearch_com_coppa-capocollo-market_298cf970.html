Meaning

Capocollo, also known as COPPA, is a type of Italian dry-cured pork salume, originating from the muscle running from the neck to the fourth or fifth rib of the pork shoulder or neck. Its production involves salting, seasoning with spices and herbs, and curing over a period of time, which results in its unique flavor and texture. The market for Capocollo includes various stakeholders such as farmers, meat processors, distributors, retailers, and consumers who appreciate gourmet and traditional food products. Understanding this market requires a comprehensive analysis of production techniques, supply chains, regulatory environments, and consumer trends.
