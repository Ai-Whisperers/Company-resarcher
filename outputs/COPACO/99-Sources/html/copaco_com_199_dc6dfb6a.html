New Copaco Order Status App makes doing business easier and more accessible

April 21st, Copaco launches the new Order Status App for partners. With this app Copaco partners can check their order status more quickly and easily at any time.

“We are very happy and proud to present our new Order Status App” says Jurgen Janssens, director of Copaco Belgium. “We are continuously trying to boost our customers satisfaction by improving our accessibility so our clients can work more efficiently. We do this based on the wishes of our partners. For example, we developed the new Copaco Order Status App so that our partners can track their orders from the moment they receive an order confirmation. Fast, easy and at any time.”

To Order Status App

Benefits

The app contains so much more than a usual Track & Trace tool which you can find below:

  Track orders quickly and easily 
  From any device
  Quick access to Track & Trace
  Direct insight into the confirmation of receipt
  Extensive order line details

How does it work?

It is very simple. Follow the steps below and you can use the app as a partner of Copaco.

  • Go to orderstatus.copaco.com
  • Log in with your Copaco account
  • Search on your personal reference or Copaco order number
  • Click to open your order for more extensive details
Tip: Add the app to your home screen. This gives you even faster access.
