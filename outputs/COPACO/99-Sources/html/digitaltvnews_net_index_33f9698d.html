December launch for Copaco and Tigo pay TV services in Paraguay

Tuesday, October 25th, 2011

Copaco has confirmed that its IPTV service will launch on December 15th in Paraguay. In the same month, Tigo, part of the Millicom Group (NASDAQ:MIICF), is expected to launch a digital cable TV service in the Paraguayan city of Lambaré.
