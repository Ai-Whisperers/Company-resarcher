Copaco: pampered by the president, whom he accompanies “in everything”, the model that everyone talks about
Sol Sánchez joined Copaco as contracted on April 18.  She currently holds the position of Head of the Protocol Unit of the institution.
According to the versions collected by the newsroom, Sánchez is seen very closely with the president of Copaco, Sante Vallese, coinciding with the creation of the Intergovernmental Affairs Unit.
Currently, Sánchez has been working for three months at Copaco, receiving a monthly salary of G. 3,480,000, but it would increase, given that the position he occupies is at the managerial level.
Sánchez was shown on several occasions sharing political meetings in which he accompanied Vallese, mainly in acts related to the campaign of the Vice President of the Republic, Hugo Velázquez.
Sánchez’s profile on her social networks indicates that she is an entrepreneur and student of International Trade, and also has the title of “Miss Latin America Paraguay 2019”.
