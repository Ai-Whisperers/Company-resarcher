Choose a CRM platform
Choosing a CRM platform that suits your telecom business needs and goals is the first step. There are many options available, but you should look for one that offers features such as integration with your telecom systems, customization and scalability to match your products and services, reporting and analytics to measure sales performance and customer satisfaction, automation and workflows to streamline processes and tasks, segmentation and targeting to identify and reach prospects and customers, as well as communication and collaboration tools to connect with your team and customers.

Import and organize your data
The next step is to import and organize your data into your CRM platform. To ensure the data is accurate, complete, and up-to-date, you should have a clear and consistent data structure that reflects your telecom sales cycle and customer journey. Some data sources that you can use include your existing customer database (e.g. contacts, accounts, and contracts), leads and opportunities (e.g. inquiries, quotes, and proposals), sales activities and history (e.g. calls, emails, and meetings), as well as marketing campaigns and channels (e.g. social media, email, and webinars).
