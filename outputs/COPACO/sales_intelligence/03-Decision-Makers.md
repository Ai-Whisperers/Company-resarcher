#  Decision Makers

**Company:** COPACO
**Industry:** Telecommunications
**Date:** 2025-12-02

---

## Decisionmakers

### Item
- **Area:** Financial Stability
- **Details:** Copaco has experienced a significant decrease in its net worth over the last three years, accumulating operational losses that have severely impacted its financial stability. As of December 31, 2023, an audit by Gestión Empresaria (GE) revealed a loss of G. 363.481 million (USD 46.4 million) in net worth from 2021 to 2023, marking a 48.6% decrease. The operational losses are primarily attributed to a loss of G. 174.848 million (USD 22.3 million) in 2023, stemming from the application of the equity participation method (VPP) due to the investment in Hola Paraguay SA, which absorbed investment accounts in other companies amounting to G. 77.967 million. Copaco also faces an accumulated debt of G. 833.000 million (USD 113 million), further exacerbating its financial challenges.
- **Source:** El Paraguayo Independiente
- **Date:** December 31, 2023

### Item
- **Area:** Leadership
- **Details:** Óscar Stark, the president of Copaco, has acknowledged that the company lacks sufficient resources to meet all its obligations and that accumulated losses have eroded its net worth. He also noted that the acquisition of Vox, which already had accumulated losses, has worsened Copaco's financial situation.
- **Source:** El Paraguayo Independiente

### Item
- **Area:** Cost Reduction Measures
- **Details:** To address the financial crisis, Copaco has implemented measures such as staff reductions and renegotiation of labor conditions to ensure long-term financial sustainability.
- **Source:** El Paraguayo Independiente

### Item
- **Area:** Internet Access Expansion
- **Details:** Copaco, in partnership with Mitic and Starlink, has begun installing satellite internet in underserved communities. The first connection was established at a school in the Chaco region, with plans to extend high-speed internet to 500 schools in digitally isolated areas during the initial phase. Speeds of up to 280 Mbps were recorded in the indigenous community of Qom de Cerrito.
- **Source:** La Nación

### Item
- **Area:** Company Transformation
- **Details:** Copaco S.A. was established on November 15, 2001, and began operations on December 3, 2001, following the transformation of ANTELCO under Law 1615/00. The company aims to provide public telecommunications services nationally and internationally, including broadcasting and value-added services.
- **Source:** Copaco S.A. - Quiénes Somos

### Item
- **Area:** LTE Network Deployment
- **Details:** Copaco launched its LTE network in Paraguay, available in Asuncion and surrounding areas. The deployment includes 109 radio bases throughout Paraguay, with 89 in the Asuncion metropolitan area, one in Pilar, five in Encarnacion, and 14 in Ciudad del Este.
- **Source:** RCR Wireless News
- **Date:** February 27, 2013
