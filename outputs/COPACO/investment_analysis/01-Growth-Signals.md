#  Growth Signals

**Company:** COPACO
**Industry:** Telecommunications
**Date:** 2025-12-02

---

## Growth Signals

### Item
- **Signal:** Financial Performance Decline
- **Details:** Copaco has experienced a significant decline in its net worth over the past three years. From 2021 to 2023, its net worth decreased from G. 749.977 million to G. 386.481 million, a 48.6% reduction.
- **Source:** Última Hora, El Paraguayo Independiente

### Item
- **Signal:** Accumulated Losses
- **Details:** Copaco's operational losses are primarily attributed to a loss of G. 174.848 million (USD 22.3 million) in 2023, stemming from the application of the equity participation method (VPP) related to the investment in Hola Paraguay SA.
- **Source:** Última Hora, El Paraguayo Independiente

### Item
- **Signal:** Debt Accumulation
- **Details:** Copaco faces an accumulated debt of G. 833,000 million (USD 113 million), exacerbating its financial challenges.
- **Source:** El Paraguayo Independiente

### Item
- **Signal:** Coverage Expansion
- **Details:** Copaco increased its fiber optic coverage 15 times in four years, expanding from approximately 20 neighborhoods in Asunción in 2018 to 300 neighborhoods across 33 cities by mid-2022.
- **Source:** DPL News

### Item
- **Signal:** Fiber Optic Infrastructure
- **Details:** Copaco has expanded its fiber optic network to over 8,300 kilometers across the country.
- **Source:** DPL News

### Item
- **Signal:** Satellite Internet Expansion
- **Details:** Copaco, in partnership with MITIC and Starlink, has begun installing satellite internet in isolated communities, starting with a school in the Chaco region. The initial phase targets 500 schools.
- **Source:** La Nación

### Item
- **Signal:** Potential Investment
- **Details:** A Saudi Arabian investment fund is considering investing USD 150 million in Copaco to revitalize the state-owned telecommunications company.
- **Source:** La Política Online, Fondo saudita invertiría USD 150 millones en Copaco

### Item
- **Signal:** Interested Parties
- **Details:** Rivada Networks has expressed interest in leasing VOX's spectrum with a potential investment of up to USD 500 million in infrastructure.
- **Source:** Fondo saudita invertiría USD 150 millones en Copaco

### Item
- **Signal:** Modernization and Restructuring
- **Details:** A bill is being considered to modernize and restructure Copaco, aiming for financial stability, technological innovation, and attracting public-private investment.
- **Source:** La Nación / Analizaron propuesta de marco legal para modernizar Copaco y atraer inversión público-privada, Diario HOY | Plantean ley para salvar Copaco

### Item
- **Signal:** Labor Disputes
- **Details:** Copaco is facing labor disputes due to unpaid salaries, with 200 of its 2,463 workers not receiving their salaries since December 2024.
- **Source:** Funcionarios de Copaco protestan por falta de pago de salarios
