#  Risk Factors

**Company:** COPACO
**Industry:** Telecommunications
**Date:** 2025-12-02

---

## Riskfactors

### Item
- **Risk:** Financial Losses
- **Details:** Copaco has experienced significant financial losses. In 2022, the company recorded a loss of G. 150.889 million (approximately US$ 20.7 million) up to November.  Losses in 2023 exceeded 95,000 million guaraníes. By 2025, the negative balance had climbed to $200 million USD.
- **Source:** DPL News, La Política Online

### Item
- **Risk:** Financial Instability
- **Details:** Copaco's financial situation is so dire that it has struggled to pay its 4,000 employees on time. Expenses (G. 447.769 million) significantly exceeded income (G. 296.879 million) in 2022.
- **Source:** DPL News

### Item
- **Risk:** Inefficient Management
- **Details:** Some officials attribute the company's losses to mismanagement and inoperability.
- **Source:** DPL News

### Item
- **Risk:** Subsidiary Losses
- **Details:** The mobile subsidiary Vox has contributed to Copaco's financial difficulties. Copaco absorbed Vox due to recurrent financial problems. Vox was in technical bankruptcy since 2021.
- **Source:** TeleSemana.com, La Política Online

### Item
- **Risk:** Integration Challenges
- **Details:** The integration of Vox into Copaco may present challenges, as Copaco had previously kept Vox separate from public entity regulations.
- **Source:** TeleSemana.com

### Item
- **Risk:** Potential Privatization
- **Details:** There is discussion of partial privatization of Copaco due to its financial struggles.
- **Source:** La Política Online

### Item
- **Risk:** Contract Renegotiation
- **Details:** There are plans to renegotiate the collective contract to address financial losses.
- **Source:** La Política Online

### Item
- **Risk:** External Factors
- **Details:** The COVID-19 pandemic has negatively impacted Copaco's revenues, according to the Procurator General of the Republic.
- **Source:** DPL News
