## Indirect Competitors / Alternatives


### Starlink

**Type:** Satellite Internet Provider

Starlink provides satellite-based internet services, targeting areas with limited or no terrestrial broadband infrastructure. Launched services in late 2023/early 2024 after receiving a CONATEL license in October 2023.

**Potential Impact:** Could affect COPACO's market share in rural or underserved areas, despite being more expensive than urban fiber plans.


### Vox

**Type:** MVNO

A Mobile Virtual Network Operator that uses Tigo’s network infrastructure to offer cheaper prepaid plans.

**Potential Impact:** Can attract price-sensitive customers away from COPACO.


### Núcleo

**Type:** MVNO

Another Mobile Virtual Network Operator operating on Personal’s network.

**Potential Impact:** Can attract price-sensitive customers away from COPACO.


## Emerging Threats


### 5G Rollout by competitors

Competitors are planning 5G auctions and network deployments. COPACO needs to keep pace with this technology upgrade.

**Timeline:** 2024-2025 auction planned

**Mitigation:** COPACO needs to invest in 5G infrastructure and spectrum licenses.


## COPACO's Competitive Advantages


### State-Owned Status

Being a state-owned company may offer COPACO certain advantages in terms of government support and access to resources.

**Sustainability:** Potentially sustainable, but depends on government policies and efficiency of operations.


## Market Dynamics

**Market Concentration:** Oligopoly, dominated by Tigo, Personal, and Claro.

**Price Competition:** High, especially among MVNOs and in prepaid segments.

**Key Differentiation Factors:**
- Network coverage
- Data speeds
- Price
- Value-added services
- Customer service

**Barriers to Entry:**
- High infrastructure costs
- Regulatory hurdles (licenses)
- Established brand presence of existing players

## Competitive Positioning Summary

COPACO faces significant competition from Tigo, Personal, and Claro, which hold a large majority of the market share. COPACO's competitive advantages may stem from its state-owned status, but it needs to invest in technology upgrades (like 5G) and improve service offerings to remain competitive. Its reliance on Personal for network access also affects its competitive position. COPACO's market rank is lower than the top 3 competitors. Key differentiators are not explicitly known and it needs to establish these to better compete. Strategic outlook is uncertain unless it can modernize and innovate.
