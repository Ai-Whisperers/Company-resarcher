## Indirect Competitors / Alternatives


### Starlink

**Type:** Satellite Internet Provider

Provides satellite-based internet services, targeting rural and underserved areas.

**Potential Impact:** Could attract customers in areas where fixed broadband is limited, impacting COPACO's potential growth in rural areas. However, the higher cost of Starlink hardware and subscriptions may limit its widespread adoption.


## Emerging Threats


### 5G Rollout

The planned auction and rollout of 5G networks could significantly improve mobile internet speeds and coverage, potentially impacting the demand for fixed broadband services offered by COPACO. The 5G auction was planned for 2024-2025 with an aim to reach 50% coverage a year after launch.

**Timeline:** Near term (1-2 years)

**Mitigation:** COPACO should invest in upgrading its fixed broadband infrastructure and explore offering bundled services with mobile operators to remain competitive.


## COPACO's Competitive Advantages


### Government Partnership for Rural Connectivity

COPACO's partnership with MITIC and Starlink to install satellite internet in isolated areas gives them an advantage in reaching underserved populations.

**Sustainability:** Moderately sustainable as long as the partnership continues and COPACO can effectively manage the deployment and maintenance of the satellite infrastructure.


## Market Dynamics

**Market Concentration:** Oligopoly, dominated by Tigo, Personal, and Claro.

**Price Competition:** Moderate. Competition exists, particularly in urban areas, but the market is not characterized by aggressive price wars.

**Key Differentiation Factors:**
- Coverage
- Speed
- Price
- Bundled Services
- Customer Service

**Barriers to Entry:**
- High infrastructure costs
- Regulatory hurdles
- Established brand presence of existing players

## Competitive Positioning Summary

COPACO holds a smaller market share compared to Tigo, Personal, and Claro. COPACO's partnerships for rural internet access and its established presence provide some differentiation. The strategic outlook depends on COPACO's ability to innovate, upgrade its infrastructure, and effectively compete in both urban and rural markets.
