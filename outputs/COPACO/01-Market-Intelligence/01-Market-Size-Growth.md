## Market Challenges

### Cable theft

Copaco lost 40,000 users in 2023 due to cable theft. The copper cables in older central units are targeted, but fiber optic cables are also cut, disrupting service. Repositioning the cables is slow, prompting a shift to fiber optics or wireless transmission.

**Severity:** High### Spectrum auction delays

The COVID-19 pandemic caused delays in upcoming spectrum auctions.

**Severity:** Medium### Closing of 2G/3G networks

Some of the 2G networks have already been closed, and all of the major 2G/3G platforms are expected to be shut by the end of 2025.

**Severity:** Medium

## Regional Insights

Paraguay is experiencing a digital divide, particularly in rural areas. COPACO is partnering with MITIC and Starlink to deploy satellite internet to bridge this gap, starting with over 500 schools in remote communities.
