## Indirect Competitors / Alternatives


### Starlink

**Type:** Satellite Internet Provider

Starlink offers satellite-based internet services, providing connectivity to areas with limited or no terrestrial broadband infrastructure. It gained a CONATEL license to operate in Paraguay in October 2023 and launched services in late 2023/early 2024.

**Potential Impact:** Starlink's service uptake is strong, especially in rural areas, presenting a challenge to traditional mobile operators like Tigo Paraguay in providing internet access to underserved regions.


### Vox

**Type:** MVNO

Vox is a Mobile Virtual Network Operator (MVNO) that uses Tigo’s network infrastructure to offer cheaper prepaid plans.

**Potential Impact:** Vox competes with Tigo Paraguay by offering cheaper prepaid plans, potentially cannibalizing some of Tigo's customer base.


### Núcleo

**Type:** MVNO

Núcleo is another Mobile Virtual Network Operator (MVNO) that operates on Personal’s network.

**Potential Impact:** Núcleo competes with Tigo Paraguay by offering alternative mobile plans, potentially attracting customers seeking different value propositions.


## Emerging Threats


### 5G Deployment by Competitors

Competitors launching 5G networks could attract subscribers seeking faster speeds and lower latency, giving them a competitive edge. Commercial 5G had not launched as of 2023, with a 2024–2025 auction planned.

**Timeline:** 2024-2025

**Mitigation:** Tigo Paraguay should accelerate its own 5G deployment plans and promote the benefits of its 5G network to retain and attract customers.


### Increased Competition from MVNOs

MVNOs like Vox and Núcleo could gain market share by offering more competitive pricing and targeted services, putting pressure on Tigo Paraguay's margins and subscriber base.

**Timeline:** Ongoing

**Mitigation:** Tigo Paraguay could develop its own MVNO strategy, offering a wider range of plans and services to compete with MVNOs, or focus on differentiating its premium services.


## Tigo Paraguay's Competitive Advantages


### Established Market Position

Tigo Paraguay has a strong market presence with approximately 2.5 million subscribers and a 30% market share. It operates 2G, 3G, and 4G/LTE networks with strong urban coverage.

**Sustainability:** Relatively sustainable, but requires continuous investment in network infrastructure, service innovation, and customer experience to maintain its market position.


## Market Dynamics

**Market Concentration:** Oligopoly, dominated by three major players: Claro, Tigo, and Personal.

**Price Competition:** High, as operators compete on pricing and plan options to attract and retain customers.

**Key Differentiation Factors:**
- Network coverage
- Data speeds
- Pricing
- Service quality
- Value-added services

**Barriers to Entry:**
- High capital expenditure for network infrastructure
- Regulatory requirements and licensing
- Established brand recognition of existing players

## Competitive Positioning Summary

Tigo Paraguay is the second-largest mobile operator in Paraguay with a 30% market share. Its key differentiators include its established market position, strong urban coverage, and wide range of mobile services. Strategically, Tigo Paraguay needs to focus on 5G deployment, service innovation, and customer experience to maintain its competitive edge in a dynamic market.
