Tigo Paraguay is the commercial brand of Telefonica Celular del Paraguay S.A. (Telecel), the first Paraguayan mobile phone company, owned by Millicom International Cellular. Here are some key points about Tigo Paraguay's mobile network:
Market Share
: Tigo Paraguay led the market in 2018 with 43.8% of the mobile share, making it the largest provider in the country.
Network Coverage
: Tigo has extensive coverage, including 2G, 3G, 4G, and 5G networks. The 4G/LTE network was introduced in 2016, and it has been expanding since then.
Awards and Recognition
: Tigo has received awards for 4G Availability and 4G Coverage Experience. Users on Tigo's network spend 92% of their time with an active 4G connection, significantly higher than those on Claro and Personal networks.
Business Plan
: Tigo Paraguay has a comprehensive 5-year business plan that includes investing in mobile access to broadband, fixed business lines, and licenses and/or acquisitions. This plan aims to support the shift from voice to data services and ensure universal and affordable broadband availability.
Financial Support
: Tigo received financial support from the Inter-American Investment Corporation (IIC) for its business plan, which includes expanding 3G and 4G-LTE networks, increasing fixed internet and digital TV coverage, and enhancing Business-to-Business (B2B) services.
Overall, Tigo Paraguay has a strong presence in the country's telecommunications market, with significant investments in modernizing its network infrastructure and expanding its services.
