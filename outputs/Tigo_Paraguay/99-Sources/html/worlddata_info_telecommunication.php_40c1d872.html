Mobile communications and Internet in Paraguay

The development of telecommunications in Paraguay is roughly equivalent to the United States. Under the country code +595, there were a total of 8.87 million connections in 2023. Among them were 8.67 million mobile phones, which corresponds to an average of 1.3 per person. In the US, this figure is 1.1 mobile phones per person.

Internet speed for cell phones and fixed network

With an average download speed of 103.51 Mbit/second for fixed-network broadband internet, Paraguay ranks 58th in an international comparison. The upload rate of only 31.42 Mbit/second was significantly lower (96th place).
In mobile internet, i.e., on tablets and smartphones, Paraguay comes 102nd with a download speed of 20.11 Mbit/second. The upload speed of around 9 Mbit was enough for 98th place.

The Speedtest Global Index published regularly by Ookla is based on several million individual measurements in October 2025 from 156 countries.

Mobile phone rates in Paraguay

Every year, the International Telecommunication Union calculates for many countries what a mobile phone contract costs to use. A monthly budget averaging US$ 8.10 was calculated Paraguay in 2025. Compared to the previous year, this amount has fallen by 43 percent.

The ITU refers to the costs for monthly use and includes 140 telephony minutes, 20 text messages and a data transfer of 5 GB. The transfer speed should be the minimum of a 3G connection, i.e. at least UMTS, which is already outdated in many parts of the world. 5 GBytes doesn't sound like much to us. However, in a global comparison, this is already a very high consumption. If you don't take a fixed amount in US dollars, but convert it to economic output per inhabitant, a user in Paraguay spends 1.5% of their income on mobile telephony. Worldwide, this rate is around 4.7 percent.

In the USA, the monthly cost was around $47.40.

Lack of network coverage with 5G

There is not yet a modern 5G network in Paraguay (as of 2023). The penetration of 4G, i.e. mobile communications with at least LTE speed, recently stood at 96.90%. Conversely, 214,803.74 people in the country had to make do with the maximum Internet speed of the outdated UMTS standard (or even less).

Broadband internet in Paraguay 2000 - 2023

Paraguay is lagging behind in the expansion of broadband internet connections. Around 82 percent of all residents have access to the internet. There are around 881,769 Internet connections nationwide that are at least faster than the former ISDN (more than 256 kbit/s). All fixed connections via DSL, cable or satellite were counted here, but not those connected to the internet via mobile communications. This would be around 13 subscriptions per hundred inhabitants, although multiple use already occurs because there is usually more than one person living in a household and people usually use another Internet connection at work.

Landlines 1990 - 2023

Mobile phones 1992 - 2023

The increasing prevalence of cell phones and the simultaneous decline in landline phones clearly indicate a change in Paraguay. While the number of landlines has fallen significantly in recent years, the spread of cell phones has risen to 127 cell phone subscriptions per 100 people at last count. Such a clear shift does not occur in all countries, but the trend points in the same direction internationally.
