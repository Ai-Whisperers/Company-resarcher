Paraguay

Highlights

  1. Internet Society gives Paraguay an overall Internet resilience score of 55%, which means it has medium capacity to withstand unexpected faults or challenges to normal operation.
  2. Paraguay received a security preparedness score of 74.93 on the 2024 Global Cybersecurity Index. Its e-government system readiness score is 62.20.
  3. As of 2024, the population of Paraguay was 6,929,153. The United Nations defines it as a land-locked developing country.
  4. Approximately 78% of the population is an Internet user.
  5. Internet Society rates the choice of Internet service providers as poor.
