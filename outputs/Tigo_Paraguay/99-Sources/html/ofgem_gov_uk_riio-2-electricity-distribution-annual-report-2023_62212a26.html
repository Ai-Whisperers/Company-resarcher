The RIIO-2 Electricity Distribution: Annual Report 2023 to 2024 gives an overview of the Electricity Distribution (ED) network operators’ (DNOs) output delivery and financial performance for the first year of the RIIO-ED2 price control.  

The report summarises DNO performance based on the information submitted in the 2023 to 2024 reporting year.
