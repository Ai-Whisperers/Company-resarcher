Green Glove Service Program

Offering for first-time installers of Tigo residential solar and storage products AND now available for both new and existing commercial installations with any MLPE equipment.
Get Green Glove

WHY TIGO GREEN GLOVE FOR COMMERCIAL & INDUSTRIAL INSTALLATIONS

Tigo has comprehensive experience with C&I design and installation challenges.

Sign up for the Green Glove program and Tigo will provide an extensive review of both planned and previously commissioned C&I installations. While focused on Tigo MLPE, we will also evaluate other rapid shutdown solutions.

OUR COMMITMENT TO SERVICE

We recognize the challenges associated with MLPE design and installation and look forward to helping you every step of the way.

For all C&I installers, the Green Glove program provides a review of existing or planned installations in the following manner:
  • Before: Three Point Design Inspection of (1) System Design, (2) Compatibility Design, (3) Installation Plan
  • During: We remain on-call at regional Tigo locations during installation
  • After: We conduct post-installation review and follow-up discussions which includes: (1) Powerflow Chart, (2) System View, (3) Electrical Charts review, and answer outstanding questions and gather feedback on the installation experience.
  • On-Site Consultation: While most work can be done remotely, we will discuss the need for a physical review of current installations

WHY TIGO GREEN GLOVE FOR RESIDENTIAL

New to Tigo? You’re not on your own.

Sign up for the Green Glove program before your first install and the Tigo support team will be with you when it matters most.

OUR COMMITMENT TO QUALITY

Enhancing the installer experience is part of Tigo's commitment to quality across the solar value chain.

For residential installers new to Tigo products, the Green Glove program provides three support touch points during the first installation:
  • Before: Tigo support personnel provide a design review prior to installation
  • During: We remain on-call at regional Tigo locations during installation
  • After: We conduct post-installation review and follow-up discussions to answer outstanding questions and gather feedback on the installation experience

Company
Tigo Energy, the worldwide leader in Flex MLPE (Module Level Power Electronics), designs innovative solar power conversion and storage products that provide customers more choice and flexibility. The Tigo TS4 platform increases solar production, decreases operating costs, and enhances safety. When combined with the Tigo Energy Intelligence (EI) platform, it delivers module, system, and fleet-level insights to maximize solar performance and minimize operating costs. The Tigo EI Residential Solar Solution, a flexible solar-plus-storage solution for home installations, rounds out the Company’s portfolio of solar energy technology. Tigo was founded in Silicon Valley in 2007 to accelerate the adoption of solar energy, and its global team supports customers whose systems reliably produce gigawatt hours of safe solar energy on seven continents.
