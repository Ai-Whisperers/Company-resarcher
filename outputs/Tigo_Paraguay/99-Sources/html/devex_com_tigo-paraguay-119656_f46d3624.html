Tigo Paraguay

    About

    Tigo Paraguay is the leading provider of mobile services, seeking to be an engine of digital inclusion and transformer of realities.

    The mobile service has a great coverage, covering 96% of the national territory and also offering international roaming in 220 countries and data services in 137 countries . In the last ten years, more than 9000 km of fiber have been deployed throughout the country.

    The award-winning Tigo Money brand, with 2.2 million active clients , provides mobile financial services, thus favoring the social and economic inclusion of thousands of people. With this service, Tigo Money customers can send money orders, pay for services and access microcredits quickly, comfortably and safely, either directly through their phones or through the more than 4,000 Tigo outlets. Money distributed throughout the country.

    Through Tigo TV and Internet Hogar, Tigo offers Pay TV and Internet to households in Paraguay. It has 124 international channelsand 53 HD channels . In addition, in 2017 we launched the Satellite TV service (DTH) which to date has more than 25,000 customers, 2 SD signals , 9 HD signals and 10 Audio signals . Tigo Business offers more than 19,000 companies a complete portfolio of solutions adapted to the needs of each business, voice, connectivity and cloud.
