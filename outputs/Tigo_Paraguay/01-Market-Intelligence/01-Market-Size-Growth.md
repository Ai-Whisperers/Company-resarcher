**Severity:** Medium### Cautious spectrum policy

**Relevance to Company:** Tigo Paraguay can capitalize on increasing internet penetration by expanding its service offerings and targeting underserved areas.### Starlink entry

**Relevance to Company:** Tigo Paraguay faces increased competition from Starlink, especially in rural areas with limited connectivity options.
## Regional Insights

Paraguay had approximately 78% of its population online in 2025, which is below the Latin American average of about 85%. Urban usage exceeds 80%, while rural usage is around 64%. 4G LTE networks cover about 97% of the population. Paraguay has at least three diverse international fiber paths, including cross-border links to Argentina and Brazil via Itaipu, providing good upstream diversity of international connectivity. Paraguay secured a $130 million IDB loan to support the Digital Agenda/Plan TIC 2022–2030.
