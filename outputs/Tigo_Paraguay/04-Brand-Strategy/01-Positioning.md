### History

Millicom International Cellular SA was established on December 14, 1990, by Shelby Bryan, Jan Stenbeck, Telma Sosa, and Olvin Galdamez. It combined the cellular telephone properties owned by Industriförvaltnings AB Kinnevik and Millicom Incorporated. Millicom operates under the Tigo and Tigo Business brands.

## Unique Selling Proposition (USP)

Largest operator in Paraguay, actively deploying 5G services in cooperation with Conatel. Example: Working with Conatel to deploy 5G services.

## Value Proposition

Digital services including high-speed data, broadband, mobile, cable TV, voice and SMS, Mobile Financial Services, and business communications.

## Brand Archetype

**Archetype:** Everyman

The Everyman archetype fits Tigo because it aims to provide essential communication services to the masses. Tigo focuses on accessibility and reliability, ensuring that everyday people can connect and communicate effectively. Their customer support via WhatsApp exemplifies this, offering easy and direct assistance.

## Positioning Statement

> Tigo Paraguay is the leading telecommunications provider in Paraguay, offering reliable and accessible digital services to connect individuals and businesses.

## Brand Strengths

### Market Leadership

Tigo is the largest operator in Paraguay. This provides a strong base of customers and resources.

**Competitive Advantage:** Greater market share and brand recognition compared to competitors, allowing for economies of scale and increased investment in infrastructure.
### 5G Deployment

Working with Conatel to deploy 5G services, indicating a forward-looking approach.

**Competitive Advantage:** Early adoption of 5G technology allows Tigo to offer faster and more reliable services, attracting customers seeking advanced connectivity.

## Brand Challenges

### 5G Licensing Delays

Initial absence from the 5G licensing process created a setback.

**Impact:** Delayed entry into the 5G market could allow competitors like Claro and Nubicom to gain a competitive edge, potentially leading to customer attrition.

## Brand Evolution

Evolved from a traditional telecommunications company to a provider of a wide range of digital services, including mobile financial services and business communications. The company is now focusing on deploying 5G services.

## Strategic Recommendations

Focus on enhancing customer experience through improved service quality and customer support. Address any negative perceptions related to service reliability. Increase transparency about pricing and service terms. Actively promote the benefits of 5G and its impact on user experience. Develop targeted marketing campaigns that highlight Tigo's unique offerings and competitive advantages.
