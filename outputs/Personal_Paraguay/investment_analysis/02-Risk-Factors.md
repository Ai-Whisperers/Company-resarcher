#  Risk Factors

**Company:** Personal Paraguay
**Industry:** Telecommunications
**Date:** 2025-12-02

---

## Riskfactors

### Item
- **Risk:** Lack of Comprehensive Data Protection Regulation
- **Description:** Paraguay lacks a comprehensive regulation for controlling and limiting the collection, storage, and use of personal data. The current legislation is specific to the credit sector, leaving a significant gap in the comprehensive protection of personal data and individual rights.
- **Source:** TEDIC - Personal Data Protection in the Private Sector in Paraguay: An exploratory study

### Item
- **Risk:** Competition from other operators
- **Description:** Personal Paraguay faces competition from other telecommunications operators in Paraguay, including Tigo Paraguay, Claro Paraguay, Copaco, and others. This competition may impact market share and profitability.
- **Source:** Dataxis - Personal Paraguay - Television and Telecom

### Item
- **Risk:** Technological Obsolescence
- **Description:** The telecommunications industry is subject to rapid technological advancements. Personal Paraguay faces the risk of its technology becoming obsolete if it does not continue to invest in and adopt new technologies such as 5G.
- **Source:** LTE en Paraguay - El Blog de Personal

### Item
- **Risk:** Cybersecurity Threats
- **Description:** Personal Paraguay, like other telecommunications companies, is vulnerable to cybersecurity threats that could compromise its network, data, and services. This is highlighted by Personal Paraguay's own cybersecurity awareness campaigns on YouTube.
- **Source:** Personal Paraguay - YouTube
