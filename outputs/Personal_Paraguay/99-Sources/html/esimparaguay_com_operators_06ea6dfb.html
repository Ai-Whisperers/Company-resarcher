2. Mobile Virtual Network Operators (MVNOs) in Paraguay

Smaller mobile virtual network operators (MVNOs) in Paraguay include:

  • Vox: Uses Tigo’s network infrastructure
  • Copaco: Rents network access from Personal
  • Núcleo: Another MVNO on Personal’s network

These smaller carriers rent the major networks to offer cheaper prepaid plans. However, they depend on the big operators for coverage meaning service quality varies.

II. How to Choose the Best Mobile Operator in Paraguay

Factors to consider when selecting Paraguay’s best mobile operator:

1. Network Coverage & Technology

  • Verify carrier offers strong coverage for areas where service is needed (work, home, travel)
  • Ensure the network provides sufficient 3G/4G speeds and capacity

2. Cost & Plan Types

  • Evaluate prepaid and postpaid plan rates
  • Assess charges for calls, texts, and mobile data
  • Consider roaming costs if traveling abroad

3. Customer Service Reputation

  • Check online reviews regarding customer support experiences
  • Consider the convenience of store locations for assistance

4. Value Added Services

  • Review if the operator offers useful extras like entertainment bundles, finance tools

Weighing needs across these areas results in the operator that offers the best fit at the fairest price.

IV. Where to Buy SIM Cards from Paraguay Mobile Operators?

Getting SIM cards from Paraguay providers is easy:

  • Claro, Personal, and Tigo run many retail stores, kiosks, and booths for walk-in purchases
  • Small carriers have select partner locations typically in Asunción
  • Online buying options also exist via operator e-commerce sites

For online orders, expect delivery in 1-3 days after submitting ID documentation unless paying extra for express shipping. So convenient opportunities exist to obtain operator SIM cards through both brick-and-mortar and internet channels.

VI. FAQs

Which operator has the cheapest plans in Paraguay?

MVNOs like Vox and Copaco offer barebones prepaid plans starting under $5 monthly. But their network service is not reliable. Tigo provides the best value for money on consistent infrastructure.

Can I use my unlocked phone from another country in Paraguay?

Yes, unlocked GSM devices should function normally by inserting a local operator SIM card assuming proper cellular and data bands are supported. Confirm phone compatibility to avoid issues.

Do Paraguay cell companies offer monthly installments on devices?

Yes, top providers let qualifying postpaid subscribers pay off nice smartphones via 12-24 monthly fee additions just like an installment plan versus full upfront cost.

What carriers have the best rural access in Paraguay?

Personal maintains widespread coverage beyond major cities while Claro focuses on urban and suburban build-outs. So Personal excels in serving rural users and remote areas.
