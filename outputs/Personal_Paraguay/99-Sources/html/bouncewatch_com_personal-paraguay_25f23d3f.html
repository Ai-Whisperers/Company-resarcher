Personal Paraguay - Company Profile

Personal Paraguay is a telecommunications company based in Paraguay, offering mobile phone and internet services. Established in 1998, the company secured the band allocation for its services through an international public tender involving reputed market players. With a rich heritage and extensive knowledge of the mobile phone business inherited from globally renowned companies like Telecom Italia and Telecom Argentina, Personal Paraguay is a subsidiary of Núcleo S.A., with ABC Comunicaciones (a Paraguayan group) owning 32.5% and Telecom Personal, a group within Telecom, consisting of W de Argentina – Inversiones S.L. and TIM (Telecom Italia Mobile) of the Telecom Italia group, holding 67.5% stake. As of now, there is no publicly available information on the company's recent investment or the parties involved in it.
