    Company Background

    Personal Paraguay, operating under the brand Personal and owned by Núcleo S.A., is a significant telecommunications provider in Paraguay. It holds approximately 26% of the mobile telephony and internet market share, making it the second-largest player after Tigo Paraguay, which commands about 42% of the market. Personal offers mobile services and has invested in international fiber routes to improve connectivity between Paraguay and global internet infrastructure. The Paraguayan telecom sector is concentrated with limited competition dominated by a few key players including Tigo, Claro (part of América Móvil), and state-owned Copaco with its mobile arm Vox. While Claro holds around 10-11% market share, Copaco's role has diminished over time due to inefficiencies and limited coverage in fixed-line DSL services. Smaller ISPs serve niche or regional markets but do not compete at scale with Personal or Tigo1.
