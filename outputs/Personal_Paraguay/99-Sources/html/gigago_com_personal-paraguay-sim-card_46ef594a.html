I. Overview about Personal – What you need to know

Personal offers a variety of services, including SIM cards for travelers in Paraguay. Here is some information you can refer to:

  • Founded year: 1998
  • Full company name: The full name of the company is “Telecom Personal Paraguay S.A.”
  • Types of services provided: Telephone cellular, Internet
  • Coverage: Personal Paraguay provides extensive coverage across Paraguay.
  • Network of stores: Personal Paraguay operates a network of retail stores and agents throughout the country to provide customer service, sell Personal SIM cards, and offer support. 
  • Customer Support: Personal Paraguay offers customer support services through various channels, including on-call support, online assistance, and in-person support at their stores. Support is typically available in Spanish, the official language of Paraguay.

Important note: While customer support is offered primarily in Spanish, customers who do not speak the language may encounter difficulties in communicating their issues or inquiries effectively.

II. Personal coverage in Paraguay

In the cities of southeastern Paraguay, like Asunción, you’ll likely have good reception with Personal’s 4G/LTE service. However, if you’re headed to the swamps, forests, and jungles of northwestern Paraguay, cell service will be spotty. You might only find 2G, 3G, or a weak 4G/LTE signal there.

Pro Tip: If your itinerary includes traveling into remote regions, consider checking coverage maps against other providers for a stress-free mobile experience in Paraguay. Compared to its competitor Claro (98.5%), Personal (97.2%) has a weaker presence in coverage. 

III. Personal speed in Paraguay

According to 2023 data, Personal’s average upload speed sits around 5.8 Mbps, while downloads average 18.1 Mbps. This suggests Personal’s download speeds might be in line with the current national average of around 18 Mbps (as of February 2024, based on Ookla’s Speedtest Global Index). 

Travelers' advice: Claro has a reputation for offering faster and more reliable internet speeds throughout Paraguay compared to Personal. If speed is your concern, Claro might be a better option due to its potential for consistently faster download and upload speeds.

IV. Does Personal Paraguay support eSIM?

No, Personal Paraguay does not currently support eSIM.  While Personal is a major mobile network operator in Paraguay with wide coverage and fast speeds, they haven’t implemented eSIM technology yet.

If you wish to utilize eSIM, you may buy it online from a reputable reseller such as Gigago. Unfortunately, Gigago does not currently sell Personal eSIM because of weaker coverage in rural regions. However, Gigago provides eSIM choices from Claro, another network operator in Paraguay.

VI. Where can you buy Personal SIM cards and eSIM?

As a traveler visiting Paraguay, being connected is essential for navigating, sharing experiences, and communicating with family and friends at home. The following guide focuses on the most convenient ways to get your Personal SIM cards and eSIM for your Paraguay journey.

1. Where to buy Personal SIM for Paraguay?

When buying a SIM card, it’s important to remember to bring your passport or valid ID for registration, which is usually required. The seller might activate the SIM card for you, or you might need to follow a simple activation process. This is a general guide, you can refer to the destinations below to buy Personal physical SIM cards upon arrival, at Personal official store and other authorized retailers.

1.1. Upon Arrival

Most major airports in Paraguay, including Silvio Pettirossi International Airport in Asunción, have kiosks operated by Personal. These kiosks let you pay for a SIM card and typically activate it right there, making sure you’re online as soon as you depart the airport. 

Note: While convenient, airport kiosks might have a limited selection of data plans compared to what you'd find in city stores, and prices might be slightly higher.

1.2. Direct from Personal official store

For a wider variety of Personal plans and potentially better deals, travelers can head to a Personal store. You can browse various data plans, ask for assistance in choosing the right one based on your needs, and get your SIM card activated by a Personal representative. 

Personal stores are easily identifiable with their signature bright blue and white colors. The storefront will usually have large signage with the Personal logo. Here are some Personal stores’ address in Paraguay:

  • Av. España 522 c, Asunción 001411, Paraguay
  • P997+9G8, Gral. Diaz Palma Nuestra Señora Santa María de la, Asunción, Paraguay

1.3. Authorized Retailers

Many authorized retailers across Paraguay sell Personal SIM cards alongside their regular products. These retailers could be convenience stores, electronics stores, etc.  While the number of options may be restricted compared to Personal official shops, it’s a convenient way to get a SIM card, especially if you’re already in a tourist area where these types of stores are easily accessible. 

2. Where to buy Paraguay Personal eSIM?

Travelers with eSIM-compatible phones have an additional option, but it’s important to note that Personal does not currently offer eSIM services.  If staying connected with Personal is a priority, you’ll need to opt for a traditional Paraguay SIM card.  

While Personal doesn’t offer eSIMs yet, several online retailers sell eSIMs for Paraguay from other providers. Simply search for “Paraguay eSIM” and head to the reseller’s website for a fully online purchase process. Gigago is a reliable option for travelers to buy Paraguay eSIM. Gigago allows you to stay connected in Paraguay with an eSIM, but using Claro’s network instead.

VII. A Better Alternatives to Personal SIM card – Paraguay eSIM

Paraguay offers a choice between three major mobile network providers: Tigo, Claro, and Personal, all with competitive rates for different budgets.

For a convenient and affordable way to stay connected with data upon arrival, consider an eSIM. Currently, eSIM options for Personal might be unavailable but there is another way to get a travel eSIM in Paraguay. Gigago, a trusted global reseller for travelers, provides Gigago Paraguay eSIM data plans on Claro’s network. 

Gigago offers various plans around $ 9.5 – $ 38.5 to match your trip duration and data usage. Prices start at a budget-friendly $ 9.5 for 1 GB of data valid for 7 days. 

Important Notice: Only newest smartphone models support eSIM technology. Before ordering, ensure that your phone is unlocked and compatible with eSIM.

VIII. FAQs about Personal SIM cards in Paraguay

Where can I buy Personal SIM cards in Paraguay?

Personal SIM cards can likely be purchased at official Personal stores and authorized retailers throughout Paraguay.

What documents do I need to present when buying Personal SIM cards in an official store?

You’ll likely need to present a valid form of identification, such as your passport, when purchasing Personal SIM cards.

Can I use my phone in Paraguay with Personal SIM cards?

In most cases, yes. However, you will need to ensure that your phone is unlocked and compatible with the frequencies used by the Personal mobile network in Paraguay. 

IX. Final words

In conclusion, Personal offers a reputable option for tourists in Paraguay, particularly those visiting cities. While Personal SIM cards’ coverage is strong in urban areas, traveling into rural or mountainous regions may result in limited service and slower connections. Be sure to research the Paraguay mobile network providers to find the best fit for your travel needs.
