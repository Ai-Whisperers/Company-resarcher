Foreigners can obtain Paraguay citizenship after living in the country for two years with a residency permit and another two years with permanent residency. In total, it takes four years to get a Paraguayan passport.

The citizenship applicant proves proficiency in the Spanish language at the A1 level or higher. Besides, foreigners must spend at least a year in the country before applying for a passport.

Close relatives, including spouses, children over 18, and parents, should apply separately. Children under 18 can only be included in an application for a residency permit or permanent residency.

The regulations for acquiring Paraguay citizenship are stipulated in the Migration Law No. 6.984/2022 of October 18th, 2022.

​Documents to apply for a Paraguay residence permit

A foreigner submits a package of documents that typically includes the following:

  • a valid passport or other identity document;

  • a visa to Paraguay, if necessary;

  • confirmation of entry into the country;

  • a certificate of marital status;

  • a certificate of clean criminal record from the applicant’s country of origin or country of

  • residence of the last 3 years — for all applicants over 14;

  • a certificate of personal data issued by Interpol — for all applicants over 14;

  • a certificate of foreigner’s personal data issued by the National Police of Paraguay — for all applicants above 14.

If documents are issued in languages other than Spanish or Portuguese, they must be translated, apostilled and notarized by the Paraguayan Consulate in the country of issuance.

​Frequently asked questions

  • Is it possible to obtain Paraguay citizenship by investment?

    No, it isn’t. A passport of Paraguay can be obtained only by naturalisation. To become a citizen of the country, one must first obtain a temporary residence permit and then a permanent one.

    The terms of obtaining residency in Paraguay as a foreigner are described on the official website of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

  • Do I have to renounce my first citizenship when obtaining a passport in Paraguay?

    Yes, Paraguay does not recognise multiple citizenship.

  • Why is Paraguay attractive to expats?

    Paraguay is a warm country with a climate similar to that of Portugal. The people of Paraguay are friendly with foreigners and are happy to include them in social activities. They invite expats to local community events, for instance, the yearly celebration of the Encarnacion Carnival.

    Among the main attractions of Paraguay are the city of Asuncion, the ruins of the Jesuit missions Jesus and Trinidad, the Lopez Palace, and the village of Maca.

    Paraguay attracts foreigners with low taxes, low cost of living and the opportunity to obtain citizenship after 3 years. With a Paraguayan passport, you can travel to 143 countries without visas.

  • What is a Paraguay Precarious Residence Card?

    A Precarious Residence Card (Carnet de Residencia Precaria) is issued to foreigners who obtain a residence permit in Paraguay. It is valid for 90 days and cannot be extended. The card’s main purpose is to allow a foreigner to live in Paraguay while the local authorities are considering their application for a residence permit.

    Also, with a Precarious Residence Card, an investor can open a bank account, obtain a tax number or apply for company registration.

    Foreigners who obtain Paraguay permanent residency by investment do not need a Precarious Residence Card.

  • What are the benefits of Paraguay permanent residency?

    Permanent residents of Paraguay can take advantage of visa-free travel to other countries in South America, low taxes, and low cost of living.

  • How long does it take to obtain Paraguay permanent residency?

    On average, applicants obtain residency in 3 months.

  • Which countries are visa-free with a Paraguay permanent residency card?

    Permanent residents of Paraguay can travel visa-free to MERCOSUR member countries. These include Argentina, Brazil, and Uruguay.

  • How much does it cost to become a citizen of Paraguay?

    One cannot buy citizenship in Paraguay. Foreigners can obtain a passport of the country only by naturalisation. First, they get a temporary residence permit for two years; then, they can obtain permanent residency. Only in another 2 years can they apply for citizenship.

  • Can you get a Paraguay passport after obtaining permanent residence in a country?

    Yes, foreigners can apply for citizenship after 4 years of holding a residence status in Paraguay.

  • Can you get dual citizenship in Paraguay?

    Paraguay does not allow its citizens to hold passports of other countries. If an investor obtains citizenship in Paraguay, they will have to give up their first passport.
