## Núcleo S.A. (TIM) Personal - Paraguay (MCC 744, MNC 05) Núcleo S.A., operating under the brand Personal, is one of the leading mobile network operators in Paraguay. As part of the larger TIM Group, Personal provides a range of telecommunications services across the country, including mobile voice, data, and SMS. Paraguay, located in the heart of South America, has a competitive mobile market, with Personal often vying for market share with other major players like Tigo and Claro. The network utilizes Mobile Country Code (MCC) **744**, which identifies Paraguay, and Mobile Network Code (MNC) **05**, uniquely identifying Núcleo S.A. (Personal) within the Paraguayan mobile landscape. Users can identify Personal on their devices and network settings using this combination of MCC and MNC codes, crucial information for troubleshooting roaming issues or manually configuring network settings. Personal has invested significantly in expanding its network coverage across Paraguay, including both urban and rural areas. Like other operators in South America, they have upgraded their infrastructure to support faster data speeds and newer technologies, such as 4G LTE. Their success in Paraguay contributes to the overall growth and development of the telecommunications sector of the country. Furthermore, the 744-05 MNC code pairing is essential for mobile device manufacturers and network engineers when setting up devices to function correctly and be compliant on Paraguay’s national Personal network. They have also been known for implementing various promotions and bundles on their mobile plans, offering more value to their customers.
