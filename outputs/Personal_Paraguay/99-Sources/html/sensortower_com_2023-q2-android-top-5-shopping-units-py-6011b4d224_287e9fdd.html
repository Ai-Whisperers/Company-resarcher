During the second quarter of 2023, the top 5 shopping apps on Android in Paraguay displayed diverse performance metrics, with download trends offering insightful data.

Tienda Personal - Paraguay saw impressive download numbers throughout the quarter. It started strong with 38.9K downloads in the last week of March, though it experienced fluctuations, dropping to 3.5K by the end of April. However, the app rebounded significantly in mid-May, reaching 18.3K downloads, and concluded the quarter with 8.6K downloads in the final week of June.

Alibaba.com - B2B marketplace registered a steady increase in downloads, peaking at around 14.1K in the first week of June. Earlier in the quarter, the app had a modest start with 5.3K downloads at the end of March, gradually rising through April and May, and stabilizing at approximately 9.8K downloads by the end of June.

SHEIN-Shopping Online also demonstrated a positive trend in downloads. Starting with 2.3K downloads in late March, the app's weekly downloads grew, reaching a peak of 4.6K in the third week of June before slightly tapering off to 4.5K in the last week of the quarter.

AliExpress - Shopping App showed more moderate download activity. It began with 2.2K downloads in the last week of March and saw a gradual increase, peaking at 4.6K downloads in mid-June. By the end of June, downloads had slightly decreased to 2.2K.

Amazon Shopping maintained a consistent performance in downloads, starting with 1.3K in late March and remaining relatively stable throughout the quarter. The app experienced a slight rise, peaking at 1.3K downloads in mid-June, and ended the quarter similarly at around 1.3K downloads.

It's worth noting that revenue data for most of these apps were not available or negligible, with Amazon Shopping managing to generate modest weekly revenues, peaking at $19 in early May.
