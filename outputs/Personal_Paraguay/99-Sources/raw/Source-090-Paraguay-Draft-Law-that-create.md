Paraguay Draft Law that creates the Legal Framework of the Telecommunications Sector - 2012
THE CONGRESS OF THE PARAGUAYAN NATION SANCTIONS FORCE
LAW:
PRELIMINARY TITLE
Article 1 .- GENERAL OBJECT.
The purpose of this Law is to establish the regulatory framework for the Telecommunications sector, its general regulations, the competition regime, user protection, and the promotion and protection of investment in the sector, as regards coverage, quality of service, efficient use of the networks and radio spectrum, as well as the powers of the State in relation to the administration and regulation of telecommunications services.
Article 2.- SPECIFIC OBJECTIVES: The specific objectives of this Law are:
Protect the rights of users of telecommunications services, ensuring efficiency, equality, continuity, quality, and access to their information, as well as guaranteeing privacy and confidentiality in communications, in accordance with the provisions of the National Constitution.
To promote healthy competition and effective competition in the telecommunications markets and, in particular, in the exploitation of networks and in the provision of telecommunications services to the public and in the supply of the resources associated with them. All this by promoting efficient investment in infrastructure and fostering innovation as a mechanism to increase the availability of services, improve their quality and ensure affordable prices.
Ensure the orderly, effective and efficient use and exploitation of the radioelectric spectrum and other limited telecommunications resources, as well as provide for their protection and safeguarding.
Promote the development of the industry that provides telecommunications products and services, through a legal framework that contains mechanisms that guarantee the principles of transparency, non-discrimination, equality and legal security.
Guarantee access to telecommunications networks and services to the public by providers and users of telecommunications services, generators and receivers of information, and providers and users of information services.
Promote research, development, modernization, technology transfer, training, and employment in the telecommunications sector, facilitating investments for this purpose.
Encourage the development of regional integration mechanisms in which the Republic of Paraguay is a party and encourage the country's participation in international telecommunications organizations.
TITULO I
GENERAL PRINCIPLES
Article 3.- REGULATION OF THE TELECOMMUNICATIONS SECTOR.
The State will guarantee that the regulation of the telecommunications sector is carried out in compliance with the principles of maximum autonomy, transparency, predictability, participation in the formulation of rules and regulations, and accountability for regulatory management.
Article 4.- REGULATORY MANAGEMENT
.
The Regulatory Entity, its organs and agents will submit their actions, acts and regulatory measures to the legal principles of legality, the primacy of the general interest, equality, purpose, rationality, proportionality, legal certainty, responsibility, transparency, efficiency, due process and morality.
Article 5.- ON THE PROTECTION OF THE RIGHTS OF CONSUMERS.
The State shall ensure adequate protection of the rights of consumers of telecommunications services. For this purpose, the State will promote those provisions, policies and plans that guarantee consumers the full exercise of their rights in accordance with the corresponding laws.
Article 6.- ON EQUITY. UNIVERSAL SERVICE AND ACCESS.
Every natural or legal person has a free and equal right of access to the use and provision of telecommunications services, subject to this law and other provisions that regulate the matter. For the full exercise of this right, the integration of the most remote places of urban centers will be promoted.
Article 7.- ON THE PROMOTION OF INVESTMENT AND INNOVATION.
The State will stimulate the expansion of networks and technological innovation in the provision of telecommunications services for the benefit of the general interest.
Article 8.- ON THE PROMOTION OF COMPETITION.
The State will create the necessary conditions for the free participation of efficient operators in the telecommunications market, the promotion of free and fair competition under clear rules, which ensure equal conditions and opportunities. Business practices that restrict free competition are prohibited, understood as, among others, agreements, parallel actions or concerted practices between companies that produce or may produce the effect of restricting, preventing or distorting competition. The new services will be provided exclusively only when this is technically or economically essential
