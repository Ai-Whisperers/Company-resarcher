Unique Selling Proposition (USP)
The Unique Selling Proposition (USP) is a clear statement that describes what makes your business different from competitors. It highlights the unique benefits or features that only your business offers, which can attract customers and give you a competitive advantage.
How to state your USP:
Identify what your business does better or differently than others.
Focus on the specific value or benefit your customers get.
Keep it clear, concise, and easy to understand.
Example:
"Our bakery uses only organic, locally sourced ingredients to create fresh, healthy pastries every day."
"We offer 24/7 customer support with guaranteed response within 30 minutes."
Your USP should answer the question:
Why should customers choose your business over others?
