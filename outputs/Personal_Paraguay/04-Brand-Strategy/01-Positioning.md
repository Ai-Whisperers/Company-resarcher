### History

Personal Paraguay has a long history and knowledge in the mobile telephony business, inherited from globally renowned companies such as Telecom Italia and Telecom Argentina. The company was awarded the band corresponding to this service in October 1997 through an international public tender.

## Unique Selling Proposition (USP)

Offers fiber optic internet with speeds up to 200Mbps, and mobile service with roaming included for some plans. Also, Personal Pay allows users to make transfers, pay with QR codes, and pay bills. They share the Download Speed Experience award with Claro, scoring between 18.1-18.9 Mbps.

## Value Proposition

Provides mobile and internet services with a focus on connectivity and customer support. Offers various plans and services to cater to different needs, including prepaid and postpaid options, fiber optic internet, and digital payment solutions like Personal Pay.

## Brand Archetype

**Archetype:** Caregiver

The Caregiver archetype is reflected in Personal Paraguay's strong emphasis on customer service and support. The brand strives to provide reliable connectivity and innovative services to meet the needs of its customers. This is evident in their efforts to offer various channels for customer support, including phone, WhatsApp, and physical offices, as well as self-service tools. The 'Auxilio: Prestame Personal' service, which lends credit to users, further exemplifies this archetype by providing assistance during times of need.

## Positioning Statement

> Personal Paraguay is a telecommunications company that delivers reliable connectivity and customer-centric services, offering a range of mobile and internet solutions, including fiber optic internet and digital payment options, to meet the diverse needs of its customers in Paraguay.

## Brand Strengths

### Customer Service

Customer reviews consistently praise Personal Paraguay for its responsive and helpful customer service team. The company offers multiple channels for customer support, including phone, WhatsApp, and physical offices.

**Competitive Advantage:** Excellent customer service enhances customer satisfaction and loyalty, differentiating Personal Paraguay from competitors who may not prioritize customer support to the same extent.
### Fiber Optic Internet

Personal Paraguay offers fiber optic internet with speeds up to 200Mbps, providing customers with fast and reliable internet connectivity.

**Competitive Advantage:** High-speed fiber optic internet attracts customers who require fast and stable internet connections for work, entertainment, and other online activities.
### Digital Payment Solutions

Personal Pay allows users to make transfers, pay with QR codes, and pay bills, offering a convenient and secure way to manage their finances.

**Competitive Advantage:** Digital payment solutions enhance customer convenience and attract users who prefer cashless transactions.

## Brand Challenges

### Market Share

Personal Paraguay holds approximately 26% of the mobile telephony and internet market share in Paraguay, ranking second to Tigo Paraguay (42%).

**Impact:** Lower market share limits Personal Paraguay's revenue and influence in the market.
### 4G Availability and Coverage

Personal Paraguay's 4G network is available but does not match the high availability and coverage of Tigo, which leads in both 4G Availability and 4G Coverage Experience.

**Impact:** Limited 4G availability and coverage may result in a less reliable mobile experience for customers in certain areas.
