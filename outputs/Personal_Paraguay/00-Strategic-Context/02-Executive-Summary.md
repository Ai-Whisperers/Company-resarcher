## Executivesummary

- **Companyname:** Personal Paraguay (Núcleo S.A.E.)
- **Industry:** Telecommunications
- **Description:** Personal Paraguay is a leading mobile and internet service provider in Paraguay, offering mobile telephony, fiber optic internet, IPTV, satellite television, and mobile financial services. It operates under the bands 850 MHz, 1900 MHz, 700 MHz, and 3500 MHz. Personal was the first network with 4G/LTE technology in Paraguay in December 2015. It also provides access to the fastest fiber optic network in the country. As of mid-2019, Personal had 2,321,290 customers, representing 31% of the market.
