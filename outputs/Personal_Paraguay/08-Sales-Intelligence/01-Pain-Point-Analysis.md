## Key Facts

- Personal arrived in Paraguay on June 24, 1998.
- Personal was the first to have a GSM network in 2004.
- Núcleo S.A. is the representative company of Personal in Paraguay, with Telecom Argentina owning 67.5% and ABC Telecomunicaciones S.A. owning 32.5%.
- In 2018, Núcleo S.A. acquired 70% of Tuves Paraguay S.A., leading to Personal TV.
- Personal offers fixed network services (Internet Hogar) and has invested in fiber optic infrastructure.
