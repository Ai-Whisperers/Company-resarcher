## Indirect Competitors / Alternatives


### Vox

**Type:** MVNO

Vox is a Mobile Virtual Network Operator (MVNO) that uses Tigo’s network infrastructure to offer cheaper prepaid plans.

**Potential Impact:** While their market share is smaller, their cheaper plans can attract price-sensitive customers, impacting Personal Paraguay's revenue from that segment.


### Copaco

**Type:** State-owned Telecom

Copaco, or Telecomunicaciones del Paraguay, is a state-owned telecommunications company that rents network access from Personal to provide mobile voice and data services.

**Potential Impact:** Copaco competes with Personal Paraguay by offering its own plans, potentially impacting Personal's subscriber base.


### Núcleo

**Type:** MVNO

Núcleo is another MVNO on Personal’s network, offering cheaper prepaid plans.

**Potential Impact:** Similar to Vox, Núcleo targets price-sensitive customers, which could lead to revenue loss for Personal Paraguay in that segment.


## Emerging Threats


### 5G Rollout Delays

Delays in 5G spectrum auctions and infrastructure deployment could hinder Personal Paraguay's ability to offer advanced services and compete effectively in the future.

**Timeline:** Ongoing, with potential for resolution within the next 1-3 years depending on regulatory decisions.

**Mitigation:** Actively engage with CONATEL to advocate for timely spectrum allocation and streamline regulatory processes. Explore partnerships for infrastructure sharing to accelerate 5G deployment.


### Increased Competition from OTT Services

Over-the-top (OTT) services like WhatsApp, Skype, and other messaging apps continue to erode traditional voice and SMS revenue streams.

**Timeline:** Ongoing and intensifying.

**Mitigation:** Develop bundled offerings that integrate OTT services with Personal Paraguay's core services. Invest in content and entertainment platforms to differentiate from pure connectivity providers.


## Personal Paraguay's Competitive Advantages


### Fiber Optic Network

Personal Paraguay has a fiber optic network, providing a strong foundation for high-speed internet services.

**Sustainability:** Relatively sustainable, as fiber optic infrastructure offers superior bandwidth and reliability compared to older technologies. However, continued investment and upgrades are needed to maintain its advantage.


### Ownership by Telecom Argentina and TIM

Being owned in part by Telecom Argentina and Telecom Italia Mobile (TIM) provides Personal Paraguay with access to international expertise, resources, and potential synergies.

**Sustainability:** Sustainable as long as the parent companies remain committed to the Paraguayan market and continue to provide support.


## Market Dynamics

**Market Concentration:** High - The Paraguay telecom MNO market is highly concentrated with three major players: Claro, Tigo, and Personal.

**Price Competition:** Moderate - The market is characterized by operator discipline focused on infrastructure sharing rather than aggressive price wars.

**Key Differentiation Factors:**
- Network coverage and quality
- Data speeds
- Service bundles (e.g., mobile, internet, pay-TV)
- Customer service
- Brand reputation

**Barriers to Entry:**
- High capital expenditure for network infrastructure
- Spectrum licensing costs
- Regulatory hurdles
- Established brand presence of existing players

## Competitive Positioning Summary

Personal Paraguay holds a significant position in the Paraguayan mobile market, with over 2 million subscribers. Its key differentiators include its fiber optic network and its association with Telecom Argentina and TIM. Its strategic outlook involves leveraging these strengths to compete effectively against Claro and Tigo, while also adapting to emerging threats such as OTT services and potential delays in 5G rollout.
