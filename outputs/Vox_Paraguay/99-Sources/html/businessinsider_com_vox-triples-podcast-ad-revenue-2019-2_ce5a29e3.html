Vox Media’s podcast ad revenue tripled year-over-year (YoY) to become a more than eight-figure business, and is on track to more than double again this year, per comments by Vox Media president Marty Moe, via Axios.

Vox Media's podcast network will help it to lure additional ad dollars through consolidation, scale, and high-profile hosts. As it doubles down on podcasting, Vox Media is in the process of building a standalone podcast network that will house and generate a large and growing portfolio of podcasts, as opposed to producing and distributing one-off podcasts.

  • Consolidation and scale: Podcast consolidation can drive content discovery and deeper traction as a result. In turn, scale can attract bigger brand spending, particularly if advertisers are interested in buying audience across more than one show in the portfolio, or across the entire portfolio. Vox is seeking to take advantage of these benefits, and has consolidated its large and still-growing group of 75 podcasts — up from 25 in 2017 — onto a single website. For context, that’s more than double the 30 podcasts within Bill Simmon’s sports-focused podcast network The Ringer, which recently said it was profitable with some $15 million in sales. 
  • Notable hosts: Further, podcasts within the network will make use of the built-in audiences attached to key personalities, and ideally convert them into new listeners to other shows within the portfolio. Vox Media’s podcasts are hosted by several key personalities like Ezra Klein and Kara Swisher, known from Vox’s various brands — including Recode and The Verge — and who have large and loyal online followings that they can direct back to the podcast network. For example, Kara Swisher has 1.29 million Twitter followers, while Ezra Klein has 2.51 million. 

Vox's podcast success shows its ability to tap into fast-growing, non-core revenue streams as it — along with the entire industry — struggles to survive on digital advertising. Last fall, Vox missed its revenue target of about $200 million in 2018 by more than 15%, amid declining digital ad sales. Since then, the publisher may have refocused around high-growth opportunities outside of digital display advertising.

For its part, the podcast medium presents a fast-growing ad opportunity: Podcast advertising rose 86% YoY in 2017 to $315 million, per the IAB. Beyond podcasting, Vox Media has also built a profitable business out of producing and licensing TV content to streaming platforms like Netflix and YouTube, as well as linear channels like CNN, through the company’s in-house studio Vox Entertainment.

Most of Vox’s entertainment deals take advantage of the growing seller’s market in OTT video, which is projected to grow 35% to $30.6 billion by 2022, per PwC. That growth opportunity has likely spurred greater activity: Vox Entertainment is developing and pitching triple the number of projects compared with spring 2018.
