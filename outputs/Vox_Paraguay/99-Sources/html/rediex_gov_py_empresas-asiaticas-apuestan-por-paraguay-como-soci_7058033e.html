Asian companies are betting on Paraguay as a strategic and sustainable partner.

Aug 6, 2025 | INFOREDIEX

The Paraguayan government is making a strong commitment to opening new markets and attracting strategic investment. Within this framework, a series of initiatives led by Rediex's commercial attaché, Paola Subeldía Blasco, and Paraguayan ambassador to Japan, Mario Toyotoshi, are generating concrete opportunities in key sectors such as the satellite industry, food, and the green economy.

These actions are part of a broader strategy to promote the country, directly promoted by President Santiago Peña Nieto. Last May, he led a trade mission to Asia, visiting Japan, Singapore, and the United Arab Emirates with the Minister of Industry and Commerce, Javier Giménez. This mission reinforced Paraguay's position as a reliable partner with high potential in innovation, sustainability, and technological cooperation.

Among the most notable achievements, the upcoming visit to the country by the Japanese company ArkEdge Space, a satellite development specialist, has been confirmed. The signing will follow a meeting with President Santiago Peña Nieto during the mission to Japan, where they held strategic meetings to explore the use and application of its technology in Paraguay, as well as to initiate efforts to establish operations in the country.

Another important milestone is the confirmed visit in September of this year by Vox Trading, a subsidiary of the House Foods conglomerate, one of Japan's largest food groups. The company will participate in Paraguay Business Week, with the goal of exploring commercial alliances with the Paraguayan agri-food sector.

On the other hand, the meat company Upisa, which has already conducted trade missions in Southeast Asian countries, has generated significant interest among major pork marketing players in the region, which could open up new export routes for Paraguayan products.

Furthermore, following the signing of the carbon credit agreement with Singapore, there has been a surge in interest in the purchase of carbon credits and investments in carbon capture projects, reaffirming Paraguay's potential as an attractive destination for the green economy and carbon markets.

It's worth noting that Paola Subeldía Blasco, Paraguay's commercial attaché to Japan, New Zealand, Australia, Singapore, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, the Philippines, Brunei, and Laos, emphasized that the trade strategy seeks to position the country as a reliable, productive, and sustainable partner.

"Our mission is to open markets, attract investment, and show the world Paraguay's potential as a green platform for business and innovation," he said.

 

Source: Industry and commerce ministry
