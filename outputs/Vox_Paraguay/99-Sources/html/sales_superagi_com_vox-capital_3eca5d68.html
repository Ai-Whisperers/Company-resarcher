VOX Capital Company Profile



Background



VOX Capital is a pioneering impact investment firm based in São Paulo, Brazil, established in 2009. The company focuses on reconciling financial returns with positive socio-environmental impact, demonstrating that these objectives can be mutually reinforcing. VOX Capital operates with integrity, rigor, and purpose to transform capital allocation, connecting investors with solutions that address significant societal challenges.

Key Strategic Focus



VOX Capital's strategic focus centers on three main areas:

  • Venture Capital: Investing in startups with the potential to scale solutions that improve lives and preserve the planet.


  • Fixed Income – Private Debt: Channeling resources through innovative financial instruments focused on socio-environmental impact.


  • Nature-Based Solutions: Supporting initiatives that regenerate ecosystems, promote climate resilience, and strengthen local economies.


The firm employs various investment instruments, including equity, OBSA, JV, and LBO, across any sector, aiming to drive scalable solutions that address major social, environmental, and economic challenges.

Financials and Funding



VOX Capital is a privately held firm that invests its own funds without seeking outside funding, ensuring a strong commitment to the success of the entrepreneurs it supports. The company has a track record of over 62 investments and 18 exits, demonstrating its ability to generate both financial returns and positive social impact.

Pipeline Development



VOX Capital's investment portfolio includes startups and businesses that generate measurable results and tangible transformations. The firm has been recognized as the only Brazilian manager selected in BNDES’ 2022 call for impact funds, highlighting its role in fostering impactful ventures.

Technological Platform and Innovation



VOX Capital leverages a proprietary methodology that combines sector screening aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), ESG risk identification, and proven impact evaluation. This approach enables the firm to assess and invest in businesses that deliver both financial returns and positive socio-environmental impact.

Leadership Team



  • Daniel Izzo: Founding Partner and CEO.


  • Gilberto Ribeiro: Partner and CIO.


Both leaders bring extensive experience in the financial market and socio-environmental impact, guiding VOX Capital's mission to transform capital into tangible change.

Competitor Profile



Market Insights and Dynamics



The impact investment sector in Brazil is growing, with increasing interest from both local and international investors seeking to generate positive social and environmental outcomes alongside financial returns. VOX Capital's pioneering role in this space positions it as a leader in reconciling return with positive impact.

Competitor Analysis



Key competitors in the Brazilian impact investment landscape include:

  • Sitawi: Focuses on social finance, providing financial services to social organizations.


  • Gera Venture: Invests in early-stage companies with scalable solutions to social problems.


  • Inseed Investimentos: Specializes in impact investing, supporting businesses that address social and environmental challenges.


These firms share a commitment to generating positive impact but may differ in their investment strategies and focus areas.

Strategic Collaborations and Partnerships



VOX Capital has been recognized as a certified B Corporation since 2015, reflecting its commitment to social and environmental performance, accountability, and transparency. The firm was also the only Brazilian manager selected in BNDES’ 2022 call for impact funds, highlighting its role in fostering impactful ventures.

Operational Insights



VOX Capital's hands-on approach involves active participation in the strategic committees of the companies it supports, providing guidance, orientation, reassurance, challenges, and support to entrepreneurs. This collaborative model ensures that investments are aligned with both financial objectives and positive social impact goals.

Strategic Opportunities and Future Directions



VOX Capital's strategic roadmap includes expanding its portfolio to include more startups and businesses that address major social, environmental, and economic challenges. The firm's strengths in impact investing and its innovative approach position it well to achieve future objectives and contribute to a more equitable and sustainable future.
