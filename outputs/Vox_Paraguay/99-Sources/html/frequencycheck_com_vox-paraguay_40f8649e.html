Vox Paraguay is a mobile network operator in Paraguay, denoted by the codes 744 01. It is part of the Compañía Paraguaya de Comunicaciones S.A. (Copaco), a state company that also provides IPTV and fixed telephony services. Vox Paraguay has a relatively small market share, with only 4% of the mobile market as of 2018.
