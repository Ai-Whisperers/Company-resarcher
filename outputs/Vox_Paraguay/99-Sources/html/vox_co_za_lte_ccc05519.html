Special Terms and Conditions

The following apply to LTE and 5G Uncapped & Capped packages

Telkom Downgrades

Downgrades of Telkom LTE package is subject to an administration fee, currently R404.00 excluding VAT.

Telkom Rollover Data

Rollover on Any-Time data until the end of the following month – Sim Only.

Telkom Fair Use Policy

Fair Usage Policy on Inclusive Data:

  • Uncapped LTE Business Hours – First 350GB data at up to 10Mbps, thereafter 50GB data at up to 4Mbps. Therafter uncapped data at up to 2Mbps for the remainder of the month.
  • Uncapped LTE All Hours 20Mbps – First 500GB data at up to 20Mbps, thereafter 50GB data at up to 4Mbps. Therafter uncapped data at up to 2Mbps for the remainder of the month.
  • Uncapped LTE All Hours 30Mbps – First 600GB data at up to 30Mbps, thereafter 50GB data at up to 4Mbps. Therafter uncapped data at up to 2Mbps for the remainder of the month.

MTN SIM Card Policies

  • The Service will only function when the SIM Card is used in an MTN approved 5G router.
  • Should the SIM Card be inserted into any device or Router that is not approved, the SIM Card will be soft-locked and disconnected from the MTN network.
  • The Fixed LTE/5G SIM Card will be locked to the cellular towers surrounding the location used when subscribing for the Service.
  • Should a Customer move the SIM Card to a different geographic location, the SIM Card will be soft-locked and disconnected from the MTN network. In such event, the Customer will need to contact the Vox Technical Service Centre to request that the SIM Card be unlocked.

Need help with LTE and 5G?

We have all the LTE and 5G answers you need

Telkom

Is my data really uncapped?

Yes, on the 1st of every month this is what you will receive for the following Telkom Uncapped:

  • Uncapped Business – 350GB up to 10Mbps, thereafter 50GB up to 4Mbps and thereafter uncapped up to 2Mbps
  • Uncapped All Hours (20Mbps) – 500GB up to 20Mbps, thereafter 50GB up to 4Mbps and thereafter uncapped up to 2Mbps
  • Uncapped All Hours (30Mbps) – 600GB up to 30Mbps, thereafter 50GB up to 4Mbps and thereafter uncapped up to 2Mbps

Does pro rata billing apply to the uncapped packages?

Yes, you will be billed pro rata for the month of activation.

What does FUP mean?

FUP means Fair Usage Policy, this is implemented to prevent overuse or abuse of services by a few users, which could lead to unavailability of services for other users.

Can I top-up my data if I run out?

Yes:

  • Capped packages can be topped up once data is depleted.
  • Uncapped 10Mbps & 20Mbps packages can be topped up to improve speed once FUP is reached.
  • Uncapped Business packages can be topped up to provide service between 7pm – 11.59pm

 

Here is a step by step guide on how to top up on the Customer Zone.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my package?

An upgrade is when you purchase a package of a higher base subscription.

A downgrade is when you purchase a package of a lower base subscription.

Both upgrades and downgrades are permitted. Downgrades are subject to a downgrade fee of R465, including VAT.

What are the hours for the Vox Uncapped LTE Business package?

12am – 7pm (An LTE/LTE-A once-off data bundle can be purchased to operate between 7pm to 11.59pm).

Is a router required to enable the service to work?

Yes. An approved router is needed and can be purchased from Vox.

Will I be signing a termed contract?

Vox Capped and Uncapped packages are month-to-month.

What RICA rules apply when purchasing Vox LTE?

Your RICA documents will be required upon ordering Vox LTE which are:

  • Certified Copy of your I.D
  • Proof of Address not older than 3 months
MTN

What hardware can I use with the Fixed LTE/5G service?

Only MTN approved Routers are accepted, these include:

  • H155-381
  • BROVI 5G H155-382
  • BROVI 5G H352-381  
  • HUAWEI 5G CPE PRO 2  
  • HUAWEI 5G CPE PRO 2  
  • ZTE 5G CPE MC801A  
  • ZTE G5C  
  • ZTE MC888 5G  
  • ZTE MC888D 5G  
  • ZTE MC889 5G  
  • NOKIA FastMile 5G Gateway 3.2 

How long does it take to get my Fixed LTE/5G SIM delivered?

We’ll do our best to have you connected within 7 working days of your order being placed.

Does my unused data roll over?

Yes it does, until the end of the following month.

How long is my Top Up data valid for?

Until the end of the following month.

How can I view my usage?

Check out the Vox Customer Zone for details on your account, and set up usage notifications via email or SMS to keep up to speed!

Are the speeds guaranteed from the Fixed LTE/5G service?

Vox Fixed LTE/5G is a best effort service and the speeds can’t be guaranteed. 

Can I upgrade my product?

Yes, the upgrade will be valid from the first (1st) day of the following month.

What RICA rules apply?

RICA documents will be required when ordering Vox Fixed LTE-A and these are:

  • A certified copy of your ID
  • Proof of your residential address – not older than 3 months

Can I move my router between different addresses?

No, Fixed LTE/5G means your SIM card is linked to the closest cellular towers that service your address.

If you do move your router, the SIM will be “soft locked” and can only be reactivated once you return to the original address.

What does FUP mean?

FUP means Fair Usage Policy, this is implemented to prevent overuse or abuse of services by a few users, which could lead to unavailability of services for other users.

What happens when I reach my FUP?

When you reach your FUP, your speed will be throttled up to 1Mbps.

Can I remove FUP?

FUP cannot be removed on Fixed MTN 5G, however you may purchase a Top up via the Customer Zone to improve your speed.
