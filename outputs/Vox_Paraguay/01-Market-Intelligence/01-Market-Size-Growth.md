## Regional Insights

The Paraguay telecom market is characterized by its land-locked cost structure, cautious spectrum policy, and a focus on infrastructure sharing. Regulatory clarity under CONATEL and the central bank’s instant-payments ecosystem encourages demand for high-availability mobile broadband. The population of Paraguay was approximately 6.1 million in 2023.

## Competitive Position

Vox is a Mobile Virtual Network Operator (MVNO) that uses Tigo’s network infrastructure. Other competitors include Claro, Tigo, Personal, Copaco, and Núcleo.
