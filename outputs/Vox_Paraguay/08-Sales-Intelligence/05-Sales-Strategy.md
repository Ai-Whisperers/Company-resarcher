## Identified Pain Points

### High Churn Rate due to contract-based discounts

Offering significant discounts (50% on 'Planes Eco' and 30% on 'Planes Mbarete') tied to lengthy contracts (up to 24 months) can lead to churn after the contract expires if customers don't see ongoing value or find better deals elsewhere. This is exacerbated by the availability of 'Números de Familia y Amigos gratuitos para hablar' which can be a strong incentive initially but loses its appeal over time.

| Business Impact | Urgency |
|-----------------|---------|
| Increased customer acquisition costs (CAC) to replace churned customers, reduced lifetime value (LTV) of customers, and potentially lower ARPU if customers downgrade to cheaper plans after the contract ends. | Medium |
### Limited Differentiation in Mass Messaging Market

Vox offers 'SMS Express' mass messaging plans. The pricing structure (tiered pricing based on SMS volume) is fairly standard. Without additional features or value-added services, Vox may struggle to differentiate itself from competitors, potentially leading to price wars and reduced margins.

| Business Impact | Urgency |
|-----------------|---------|
| Lower profitability on mass messaging services, difficulty in acquiring and retaining large enterprise clients, and vulnerability to competition from more innovative messaging platforms. | Low |
### Lack of Agility and Scalability in Corporate VPN Solutions

Vox offers 'Planes VPN' for corporate clients, but the plans are based on fixed data volumes (2GB to 10GB) or 'VPN ILIMIT' at 10GB which may not be sufficient for all business needs and could hinder scalability. Also the contract duration is 18 months which is a long time.

| Business Impact | Urgency |
|-----------------|---------|
| Limited appeal to businesses with fluctuating bandwidth needs, potential for customer dissatisfaction due to data overage charges or throttling, and difficulty in competing with more flexible and scalable VPN solutions. | Medium |

## Recommended Solutions

### AI-Powered Churn Prediction and Prevention

**Addresses:** High Churn Rate due to contract-based discounts

**Rationale:** By implementing an AI-powered churn prediction model, Vox can identify customers at high risk of churning *before* their contracts expire. This allows them to proactively engage with these customers, offering personalized incentives (e.g., bonus data, upgraded services, loyalty rewards) to encourage them to renew. The model can analyze usage patterns, billing history, and customer demographics to identify key churn indicators. This moves beyond generic discounts to targeted retention strategies.

**Value Proposition:** Reduce churn rate by X% (quantifiable based on historical data), increase customer lifetime value (LTV) by Y%, and improve customer satisfaction scores by Z%.

**Pitch Angle:** Proactively reduce churn and maximize customer lifetime value with AI-powered retention strategies.

**Objection Handling:**
- Objection: 'We already have a churn management system.' Response: 'Our AI-powered solution provides more accurate predictions and personalized interventions compared to traditional rule-based systems.'
- Objection: 'Implementing AI is too complex and expensive.' Response: 'We offer a managed service with a proven track record of successful deployments in similar MVNO environments. We can demonstrate a clear ROI within a short timeframe.'

**Success Metrics:**
- Churn rate reduction
- Customer lifetime value (LTV)
- Customer satisfaction scores (CSAT)
- Retention rate
- ROI of retention campaigns

**Implementation Approach:** Phased implementation, starting with a pilot program targeting a specific customer segment. Integration with existing billing and CRM systems. Ongoing model training and optimization.

---
### Advanced Messaging Platform with Rich Communication Services (RCS)

**Addresses:** Limited Differentiation in Mass Messaging Market

**Rationale:** Upgrade Vox's 'SMS Express' offering to a full-fledged messaging platform with RCS capabilities. This allows businesses to send richer, more engaging messages with images, videos, and interactive elements. This goes beyond basic SMS and provides a more compelling communication channel for marketing, customer service, and transactional messaging. This includes features like chatbots, personalized messaging flows, and detailed analytics.

**Value Proposition:** Increase engagement rates by X%, improve customer satisfaction with messaging by Y%, and generate Z% more leads through targeted messaging campaigns.

**Pitch Angle:** Transform your customer communication with a powerful messaging platform that goes beyond SMS.

**Objection Handling:**
- Objection: 'RCS adoption is still limited.' Response: 'We provide a hybrid solution that supports both RCS and SMS, ensuring compatibility with all devices. As RCS adoption grows, you'll be well-positioned to take advantage of its advanced features.'
- Objection: 'This is too expensive.' Response: 'Our platform offers a flexible pricing model based on usage, ensuring you only pay for what you need. The increased engagement and conversion rates will generate a significant ROI.'

**Success Metrics:**
- Message open rates
- Click-through rates
- Conversion rates
- Customer satisfaction with messaging
- ROI of messaging campaigns

**Implementation Approach:** API integration with existing systems. Training and support for Vox's sales and marketing teams. Gradual rollout of RCS features to customers.

---
### SD-WAN Solution for Corporate VPN Replacement

**Addresses:** Lack of Agility and Scalability in Corporate VPN Solutions

**Rationale:** Replace Vox's fixed-volume 'Planes VPN' with a software-defined wide area network (SD-WAN) solution. SD-WAN provides a more flexible, scalable, and cost-effective way for businesses to connect their offices and remote workers. It allows for dynamic bandwidth allocation, intelligent traffic routing, and centralized management. This addresses the limitations of the current VPN plans and provides a more compelling offering for corporate clients. Provides the ability to prioritise traffic types.

**Value Proposition:** Reduce VPN costs by X%, improve network performance by Y%, and increase network uptime by Z%.

**Pitch Angle:** Future-proof your corporate network with a flexible and scalable SD-WAN solution.

**Objection Handling:**
- Objection: 'SD-WAN is too complex to manage.' Response: 'Our solution is designed for ease of use, with a centralized management console and automated configuration. We also provide ongoing support and training.'
- Objection: 'We already have a VPN solution in place.' Response: 'SD-WAN offers significant advantages over traditional VPNs in terms of performance, scalability, and cost. We can conduct a proof-of-concept to demonstrate the benefits.'

**Success Metrics:**
- Network performance (latency, jitter, packet loss)
- Network uptime
- VPN costs
- Bandwidth utilization
- Customer satisfaction with network performance

**Implementation Approach:** Proof-of-concept deployment with a key corporate client. Gradual migration of existing VPN users to the SD-WAN platform. Integration with existing network infrastructure.
