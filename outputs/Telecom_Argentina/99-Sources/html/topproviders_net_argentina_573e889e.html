Top 5 Internet Providers in Argentina

1. Fibertel (Cablevisión)

Speed: Up to 1Gbps (Fiber) | Avg. 100 Mbps (Cable)

Coverage: Nationwide (Best in Buenos Aires, Córdoba, Mendoza)

Pros:

  • Largest cable internet network
  • Bundles with Flow TV service
  • Good customer support

Cons:

  • Congestion during peak hours
  • Price increases after promotions

2. Movistar

Speed: Up to 300Mbps (Fiber) | Avg. 50 Mbps (ADSL)

Coverage: Strong in urban areas, expanding fiber network

Pros:

  • Competitive pricing
  • Good mobile-internet bundles
  • Reliable service

Cons:

  • Limited fiber availability
  • ADSL speeds vary by location

3. Personal (Telecom)

Speed: Up to 500Mbps (Fiber) | Avg. 80 Mbps (Cable)

Coverage: Northern regions (Buenos Aires, Córdoba, Santa Fe)

Pros:

  • Fastest fiber speeds available
  • Excellent for gaming
  • Good bundle options

Cons:

  • Limited coverage outside main cities
  • Higher prices than competitors

4. Claro

Speed: Up to 200Mbps (Fiber) | Avg. 30 Mbps (ADSL)

Coverage: Nationwide (Best in urban centers)

Pros:

  • Affordable entry-level plans
  • Good mobile data bundles
  • Wide availability

Cons:

  • Inconsistent speeds
  • Customer service complaints

5. Iplan

Speed: Up to 1Gbps (Fiber)

Coverage: Limited to Buenos Aires and select cities

Pros:

  • Premium service quality
  • No data caps
  • Excellent for businesses

Cons:

  • Limited availability
  • Higher prices

How to Choose the Best ISP in Argentina

  1. Check availability in your exact location (varies by building)
  2. Consider your usage:
    • Streaming: Minimum 50Mbps recommended
    • Gaming: Fiber with low latency preferred
    • Basic use: ADSL may suffice
  3. Compare promotions (typical contracts are 12-24 months)
  4. Read recent reviews about service in your area

Final Recommendations

  • Best Overall: Fibertel (Best balance of speed and coverage)
  • Best Fiber: Personal (Where available)
  • Best Budget: Claro (Basic plans)
  • Premium Service: Iplan (Buenos Aires only)

Important Considerations

  • Prices are typically quoted in Argentine pesos but may adjust with inflation
  • Installation fees often apply (can be waived during promotions)
  • Service quality varies significantly by neighborhood
