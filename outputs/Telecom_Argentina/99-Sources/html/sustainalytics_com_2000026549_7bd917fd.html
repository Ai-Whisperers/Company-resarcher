About Our Framework

1. Total Exposure

 Initiating with a comprehensive evaluation, we determine exposure to each material ESG issue at the subindustry level, providing a holistic view of potential risks.

2. Manageable Risk

Assessing the landscape, we identify portions of a company's risk that can be effectively managed through dedicated ESG programs and policies, ensuring proactive risk mitigation.

3. Unmanageable Risk

Acknowledging industry-specific challenges, we recognize and factor out elements of risk that are inherently unmanageable for certain companies. For instance, an oil company's challenges related to carbon emissions are considered separately.

4. Managed Risk

Evaluating the controllable segment of risk, a company's performance is reflected through its policies, programs, practices, and quantitative performance measures, providing a clear understanding of risk management efficacy.

5. Management Gap

The management gap sheds light on areas of risk that remain unmanaged. Controversies impact the company's management score, acting as indicators of potential shortcomings in existing programs and policies.

6. Unmanaged Risk

Culminating the assessment, a company's ESG Risk Ratings is calculated by aggregating the unmanaged risk for each material ESG issue, providing a comprehensive overview of its ESG risk profile.
