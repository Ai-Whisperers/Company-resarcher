Telecom Argentina company overview and more information

Telecom Argentina SA (Telecom), a subsidiary of Cablevision Holding SA, is a telecommunications company that provides fixed-line and mobile telecommunication services, international long-distance services and data transmission and Internet services. The company offers plans such as total country full and equipment including Panasonic KXTG1711. Its service includes pack super classic, pack mini classic and internal maintenance services. Telecom offers a wide range of solutions to individuals, households and businesses. The company offers an investment plan and conversion of telecommunications in the country with basic telephony as an initial service through TELECOM and associated trademarks PERSONAL and ARNET. It also participates in various areas of the telecommunications market throughout the national territory. Telecom is headquartered in Buenos Aires, Argentina.
