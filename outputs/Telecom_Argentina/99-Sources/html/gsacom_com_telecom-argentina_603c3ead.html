• Telecom Argentina

    • We are Telecom Argentina, a company in constant movement with the purpose of empowering the lives of our 33 million customers through technology. Our solutions are designed to help people and communities grow and move forward, but also enjoy, be entertained and connect whenever and however they want.

      We offer an integral experience with our ecosystem of digital services and solutions. With Personal, we connect our clients at all places and all times. With Flow, we provide TV and streaming content. With our virtual wallet Personal Pay, we connect people with their money. And with Telecom, we help thousands of companies and institutions in their digital transformation. We are present with our comprehensive service proposal in Argentina and Paraguay, pay television in Uruguay and digital services in Chile.
      We are one of the main employers in Argentina, with collaborators all throughout the country who choose to be part of this great place to work. We promote the digital economy and lead an industry that constitutes a fundamental pillar for the development of society, with constant investment, sustainable practices and initiatives that promote talent and digital inclusion.

      We take pride in being active participants in society’s evolution and in continuing to enhance the world of our customers. Far from stopping, we look forward. We are the present and the future. We are Telecom, we empower your desire to move forward.
