    Telecom Argentina Uses Sovereign Bonds for $198 Million Dividend in Kind

    Nov 10, 2025
    TEO

    Summary

    Telecom Argentina’s Board of Directors authorized a significant return of capital to shareholders, resolving a dividend distribution featuring a dual-currency structure funded by a partial withdrawal from corporate reserves.

    Funding the distribution involved withdrawing ARS 220.5 billion from the “Discretionary reserve to maintain the level of investments in capital goods and the current solvency level of the Company.” This large reserve release suggests management's view that the current capital and solvency levels are sufficient, allowing for the return of capital previously earmarked for future investments.

    The distribution itself is notable for its composition. The company will execute a dividend in kind, distributing Global Bonds of the Argentine Republic (GD30) denominated in U.S. Dollars, maturing in 2030. The total nominal value of these bonds being distributed is nearly $198 million, at a ratio of $0.091837117 nominal value per share. The immediate distribution of a USD-denominated asset—rather than just local currency cash—is an impactful move in the Argentine context, providing shareholders with direct exposure to a non-peso asset held in the company’s portfolio.

    In addition to the bonds, the Board resolved a cash dividend of ARS 29.4 billion, or ARS 13.632047274 per share. Telecom Argentina provides fixed and mobile telecommunications services, pay-TV, and internet access across Argentina. The company stated this cash distribution is mainly applied to recovering the amount paid by the Company related to the Personal Assets Tax for the 2024 fiscal year. This link suggests a tax-optimized capital return mechanism for local investors.

    The dividends, corresponding to earnings generated from January 1, 2018, are subject to a 7% income tax withholding where applicable, in accordance with Argentine law.

    Dividend Distribution and Reserve Withdrawal FAQ

    • Why is this newsworthy?

      This resolution is highly newsworthy because it signals a significant, non-routine change in the company's capital allocation and dividend policy. The Board resolved to partially withdraw ARS 220,482,202,397 from a key discretionary reserve intended for capital investments and solvency maintenance. This funding supports a large, multi-faceted dividend, including an unusual distribution in-kind totaling USD 197,788,498 nominal value of 2030 Global Bonds (GD30), available November 14, 2025. Additionally, a cash dividend of ARS 29,359,176,779 is scheduled for November 25, 2025, primarily to recover Personal Assets Tax paid for the 2024 fiscal year. The magnitude and complexity of the payout, combined with the use of substantial investment reserves, make this event highly material.

    • What amount did the Board withdraw from corporate reserves to fund the distribution?

      The Board withdrew ARS 220,482,202,397 (roughly ARS 220.5 billion) from the “Discretionary reserve to maintain the level of investments in capital goods and the current solvency level of the Company.”

    • What is the key non-cash asset distributed as a dividend?

      Global Bonds of the Argentine Republic denominated in U.S. Dollars, maturing on July 9, 2030 (GD30).

    • What is the total nominal value of the US Dollar-denominated bonds being distributed?

      USD 197,788,498.

    • What is the distribution ratio for the bonds?

      USD 0.091837117 nominal value of 2030 Global Bonds per share.

    • What is the amount and ratio of the cash dividend?

      ARS 29,359,176,779 (roughly ARS 29.4 billion), at a ratio of ARS 13.632047274 per share.

    • What is the strategic purpose of the cash dividend distribution?

      It will be mainly applied to the recovery of the amount the Company paid in respect of the Personal Assets Tax for the 2024 fiscal year.

    • What is the tax implication for these dividends?

      They shall be subject, where applicable, to a 7% income tax withholding, as they correspond to earnings generated as of January 1, 2018, pursuant to Law No. 27,430.

    • When are the settlement dates for the dividends?

      The 2030 Global Bonds will be made available immediately, with settlement on November 14, 2025. The cash dividend will be made available on November 25, 2025.
