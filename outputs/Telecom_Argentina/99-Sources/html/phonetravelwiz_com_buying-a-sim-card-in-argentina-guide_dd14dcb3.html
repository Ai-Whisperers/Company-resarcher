Top-Up, Recharge & Balance Check – Claro Argentina

Claro top-up vouchers are sold in Claro stores, grocery stores, corner shops & electronics shops throughout Argentina.

To top up your Claro SIM card, text the VoucherCode to 444.

For example, if your voucher code is 123456, you would text 123456 to 444.

You can also tell the salesperson how much credit you want, your phone number, pay, and they will top up your SIM card with the POS system.

You can also recharge online with (international) debit and credit cards.

Dial *611# or text balance to 611 to check your Claro balance.

You can also top up and check your balance on the Mi Claro Argentina (My Claro Argentina) app (Android/iOS).
