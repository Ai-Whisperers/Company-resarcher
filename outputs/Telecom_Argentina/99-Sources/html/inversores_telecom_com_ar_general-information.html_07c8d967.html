We are Telecom

Telecom Argentina is a leading telco and entertainment company, having more than 21.000 employees and more than 30 millon clients around the country. We also have operations both in Paraguay and Uruguay.

We offer to said clients a both fixed and mobile internet with very high speeds and a vast platform of life and on demand contents such as music, videos, films, gaming, and TV.

We are leaders in an industry which is crucial for the economic and social development of Argentina. We also follow high environmental and social practices which are key for the development of our population.

Telecom is under and aggressive cultural and digital change which will end up transforming and making our company more competitive, leading by innovation and facing the challenges that the changes in our industry and community are generating.
