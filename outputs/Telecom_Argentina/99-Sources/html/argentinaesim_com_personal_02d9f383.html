I. Quick Facts about Personal Argentina

  • Founded: 1989

  • Subscribers: 20+ million (as of 2021)
  • Coverage: 99% population coverage on 2G, 97% on 3G and 88% on 4G/LTE
  • Network Stores & Agents: 971 Personal stores, 8500+ agents (2020)

Telecom Personal, commonly known as Personal, is the mobile arm of Telecom Argentina. It is the largest mobile network operator in Argentina with over 20 million mobile subscribers. Personal provides 2G, 3G, 4G/LTE and 5G mobile network services. The company has near nationwide coverage reaching 99% of the population on 2G, 97% on 3G and 88% on 4G/LTE.

II. Personal Argentina Coverage and Speed

1. Personal Coverage in Argentina

With close to 100% population coverage, Personal has an extensive mobile network reaching all corners of Argentina. Its 2G network covers 99% of the population. The 3G network covers 97% while its 4G reaches about 88% of the populace.

Personal has rolled out 4G services to all provinces of Argentina. With continuous expansion and modernization, the Personal 4G network is available in cities like Buenos Aires, Cordoba, Rosario, Mendoza, Tucuman, Salta etc.

5G coverage is limited at the moment but is growing. As of 2022, Personal 5G is live in parts of Buenos Aires, Rosario, Mar del Plata and Cordoba. More locations will be covered in future.

2. Personal Argentina speed

Overall, Personal demonstrated the fastest mobile network experiences in Argentina according to the metrics analyzed in the June 2023 Opensignal report. In particular, Personal was found to offer the best speeds, video experience, and consistency for applications like web browsing, emails, social media, and HD video streaming on its 4G network.

Personal users observed average 4G download speeds of 25.8 Mbps across Argentina. The average 4G upload speed for Personal was measured to be 6.9 Mbps.

In terms of 5G speeds, the report did not provide specific speed data. However, it noted that while Personal had launched 5G services using dynamic spectrum sharing, the operator’s 5G network was still in the development stage with 180 cell sites currently.

III. Personal connectivity options for travelers to Argentina

Personal offers several connectivity options for visitors to Argentina:

  • Personal Argentina SIM card – Physical SIM you can insert into your unlocked phone to access Personal’s network. Available as prepaid or postpaid.
  • Personal Argentina eSIM – Digital eSIM profile that can be activated on your phone to use Personal service if your device supports eSIM. Prepaid only.
  • Personal Pocket WiFi – Portable WiFi hotspot device with a Personal SIM to provide data connectivity to your phones, laptops etc.
  • Personal Internet Prepago – Prepaid Internet SIM card for use in tablets, cellular modems and other cellular devices. Provides data only.

Getting a local Personal SIM or eSIM is the most convenient and cost-effective option to stay connected through your trip to Argentina. The prepaid SIM cards and data packages are very affordable.

VII. How to activate Personal Argentina SIM Card/eSIM

1. How to use a Personal Argentina SIM card

Activating a new Personal Prepago SIM card is easy:

  • Insert the Personal SIM into your unlocked phone. Restart if prompted.
  • The SIM will automatically configure initial settings and acquire a local phone number.
  • You can make calls, and send texts to local numbers using the default credit on your SIM.
  • To add mobile data, go to a Personal store or shop that sells Personal top-ups and purchase a data recharge. Provide your phone number and pay.
  • Enter the code received via SMS to activate your data pack. The 4G/LTE data will automatically start working.
  • You can recharge more credit or data easily using vouchers, the Personal app, USSD codes and other methods.

2. How to activate Personal Argentina eSIM

Follow these steps to use Personal Argentina eSIM on your phone:

  • Make sure your phone supports eSIM and has the eSIM profile downloaded.
  • Go to eSIM settings on your phone and choose the Personal Argentina eSIM to activate it.
  • Follow on-screen prompts and enter any details like plan name or activation code if required.
  • Personal Argentina eSIM should activate within a few minutes.
  • You can now use mobile data, make calls, and send SMS as per your plan allowance.
  • Recharge data packs or top-up credit when required by purchasing add-ons through the app/website you used to buy the eSIM.

XII. FAQs about Personal Argentina SIM Cards

Here are some common questions about using Personal Argentina:

Does Personal have stores at Buenos Aires airport?

Yes, Personal has stores at both Ezeiza International Airport and Aeroparque Domestic Airport in Buenos Aires. You can easily buy a prepaid SIM on arrival.

Can I use Personal Argentina SIM Cards in other South American countries?

Personal SIM works only in Argentina. But you can enable international roaming to use it outside on partner networks. Additional charges apply.

Does Personal block VOIP and messaging apps?

Personal does not typically restrict apps like WhatsApp, FaceTime etc. Some peer-to-peer apps may be blocked on prepaid SIMs.

Where can I find APN settings for Personal?

APN for Personal Argentina is e-personal.com.ar. Username and Password are optional.

Can I use Personal Argentina SIM cards in unlocked foreign phones?

Yes, Personal SIMs work on unlocked phones from USA, Canada, Europe and most regions. Check compatibility.
