**Significance of the SEC Filing:**
Telecom Argentina SA, a leading telecommunications company in Argentina, has submitted a Form 6-K to the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC). Telecom Argentina SA is a prominent telecommunications company in Argentina, offering a wide range of services including fixed-line and mobile telephony, internet, and television services. For more information about Telecom Argentina SA, please visit their official website.

**SEC Form Type:**
The SEC filing submitted by Telecom Argentina SA is a Form 6-K. This form is used by foreign private issuers to report any material information that is required to be disclosed to shareholders. It allows companies like Telecom Argentina SA to provide updates and important details to the SEC in a timely manner, ensuring transparency and compliance with regulatory requirements.
