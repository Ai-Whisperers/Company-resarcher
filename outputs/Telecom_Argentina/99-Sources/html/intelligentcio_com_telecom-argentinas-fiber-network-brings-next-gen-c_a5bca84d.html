Telecom Argentina’s fiber network brings next-gen connectivity

Mark Bowen | 28 June, 2025

Telecom Argentina has completed a major infrastructure project in the city of La Paz, Entre Ríos, with an investment of over 466 million pesos.

The project is intended to expand the fiber optic network to the home (FTTH). This project covers approximately 410 blocks and seeks to enhance connectivity, entertainment and digital payment solutions in the city, supported by Personal, Flow and Personal Pay.

The announcement was made during a meeting with La Paz Mayor Walter Martin, along with municipal authorities and Telecom executives. During the meeting, details of the new network’s deployment and the benefits it will bring to the city’s residents were presented.

“We celebrate the completion of this project, which represents a substantial improvement in our city’s connectivity. Technology is a key tool for community development, and these types of investments generate a positive impact both on daily life and on long-term growth,” said the mayor.

Alejandro Saavedra, the company’s Institutional Affairs representative, emphasized: “We are supporting La Paz’s digital advancement with cutting-edge technology, providing a better experience for those who study, work or enjoy entertainment from their homes. This project is part of our commitment to the country’s Digital Transformation.”

Thanks to new FTTH (Fiber to the Home) network deployments, the city of La Paz has extensive Internet coverage with speeds between 100 and 1000 Mbps.

The high capacity of FTTH technology allows for the simultaneous enjoyment of multimedia services such as HD and 4K UHD video on multiple devices. This includes access to Flow in all its forms and enhanced capabilities for entrepreneurs and businesses.
