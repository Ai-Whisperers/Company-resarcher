Telecom Argentina S.A.'s reported carbon emissions

In 2022, Telecom Argentina S.A. reported total carbon emissions of approximately 210,000,000 kg CO2e, comprising 37,877,360 kg CO2e from Scope 1, 168,781,320 kg CO2e from Scope 2, and 580,407,160 kg CO2e from Scope 3 emissions. Notably, the Scope 1 emissions included mobile combustion (10,557,950 kg CO2e), fugitive emissions (13,583,140 kg CO2e), and stationary combustion (942,580 kg CO2e). The company also generated about 927,180 kg CO2e from waste in its operations. Comparatively, in 2021, Telecom Argentina's emissions were approximately 203,000,000 kg CO2e, with Scope 1 emissions at 25,875,320 kg CO2e and Scope 2 emissions at 203,353,800 kg CO2e, indicating a slight increase in emissions year-on-year. Telecom Argentina S.A. has not publicly committed to specific reduction targets or initiatives, as indicated by the absence of documented reduction targets or climate pledges. The emissions data is cascaded from the parent organization, Telecom Argentina S.A., reflecting a merged entity structure. The company is actively engaged in sustainability reporting, but further details on specific climate commitments or initiatives are not available.
