Telecom Argentina is an Argentinian telecommunications company that operates fixed-line, mobile and broadband telecommunications throughout the country, using the brands Personal for mobile services and Flow (formerly Cablevisión) for television services. It also operates in Paraguay and Uruguay.

1990–1998[]

Following the privatization of the National Telecommunications Company (ENTel), which was divided into two regions, Telecom Argentina was founded.
