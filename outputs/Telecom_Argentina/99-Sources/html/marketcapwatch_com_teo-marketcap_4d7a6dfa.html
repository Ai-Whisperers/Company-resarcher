Company Profile

Company Overview

Telecom Argentina S.A., along with its subsidiaries, is a leading provider of telecommunications services in Argentina. The company was founded in 1979 and is headquartered in Buenos Aires. Formerly known as Cablevisión S.A., it rebranded as Telecom Argentina S.A. in January 2018.

Core Services

  • Mobile Telecommunications: Voice communications, high-speed mobile internet, content streaming, and device sales (handsets, modems, smartwatches).
  • Internet Connectivity: VPN services, traditional IP links, and related products.
  • Cable Television: Programming and supplementary cable TV services.
  • Telephone Services: Local, domestic, and international long-distance calling, along with features like call waiting, caller ID, and voicemail.

Additional Offerings

  • Data & Infrastructure: Data transmission, virtual private networks, videoconferencing, and international signal transport.
  • Digital Solutions: Personal Pay (digital wallet), cybersecurity, and ICT services.
  • Broadcasting & Security: Closed-circuit TV, community TV, and security solutions.
  • Consulting & Investments: Advisory and financial services.
