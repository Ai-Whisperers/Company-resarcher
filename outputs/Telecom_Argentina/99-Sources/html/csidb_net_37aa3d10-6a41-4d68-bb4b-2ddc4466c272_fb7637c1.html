Profile

Telecom Argentina is a major internet service provider in Argentina, with its headquarters located in the country. The company has been involved in a significant cyber incident in the past, specifically a ransomware attack attributed to the REvil (Sodinokibi) gang in July 2020. During this attack, the attackers gained control of an internal Domain Admin account, spreading malware to over 18,000 workstations. Despite the severity of the attack, internet connectivity and fixed telephony services were not affected, although official websites were taken down. The attackers demanded a $7.5 million ransom in Monero cryptocurrency, threatening to double the amount if not paid within three days.

The company's primary URL is https://www.telecom.com.ar/, which is the website that was taken down during the ransomware attack. Telecom Argentina's HQ location is in Argentina, with the country code AR. The company's NAICS industry classification is 51, which falls under the category of Information, a sector that includes businesses involved in the creation, dissemination, and distribution of information and cultural products.

Telecom Argentina's STIX industry classification is communications, which is a sector that includes businesses involved in the transmission, reception, and processing of information. The company is known by two aliases, Telecom Argentina S.A. and Telecom Argentina, with the latter being the primary name. The company's primary URL and aliases were confirmed through publicly available information, including the company's website and online directories.

Argentina
 A ransomware attack by the REvil gang targeted a major Argentinian ISP, encrypting over 18,000 workstations and causing significant internal network damage while leaving customer services operational but disrupting corporate websites. The attackers compromised a Domain Admin account to deploy ransomware, demanded $7.5 million in Monero with a deadline to double the amount, and were suspected to have entered via a malicious email attachment, contrary to their typical exploitation of unpatched network equipment; the organization detected the intrusion early, issued internal warnings to limit network access, and the group had previously attacked another telecommunications provider.
