Telecom Argentina SA (Telecom), a subsidiary of Cablevision Holding SA, is a telecommunications company. It provides fixed-line and mobile telecommunication services, international long-distance services, and data transmission and Internet services. Telecom’s products include connectivity, cloud and infrastructure, platform, and software (SaaS). The company also provide remote monitoring and maintenance services. Telecom offers solutions to individuals, households, and businesses. It offers an investment plan and conversion of telecommunications in the country with basic telephony as an initial service. Telecom is headquartered in Buenos Aires, Argentina.
