Telecom Argentina powers transformation by focusing on customer experience

Telecom Argentina has focused its digital transformation on delivering exceptional customer experiences. This has involved investing in its network, driving collaboration through its teams, and changing the way it measures success – taking a customer-centric perspective to network experience. At the heart of it is a “one team, one metric” approach.
