Business And Financial Highlights
  • EBITDA Margin Improvement: Telecom Argentina achieved an EBITDA margin of 28.1% during fiscal year 2023, showing improvement due to effective cost management initiatives and better inflation pass-through to revenues.
  • CapEx Execution: Despite import restrictions in Argentina causing delays, the company successfully executed its CapEx plan of approximately $598 million, focusing on expanding FTTH technology, mobile network, and developing 5G.
  • Cash Flow Generation: The company generated around $400 million in free cash flow before dividends and interest payments, showing a strong cash flow generation despite challenges.
  • Subscriber Base Growth: Mobile subscriber base increased by almost 4% year-over-year, with mobile data usage growing by 11%. FTTH accesses in broadband grew rapidly, stabilizing the customer base, while the pay TV business continued to grow in Paraguay.
  • Financial Services Expansion: Personal Pay, the company's FinTech arm, achieved a significant market position with over 2 million onboarded clients by December 2023.
  • Debt Management: Telecom Argentina successfully paid down or refinanced all debt maturities during the year, improving the debt composition.

  • 5G Rollout: The company acquired 100 megahertz of spectrum in the 3.5 gigahertz band and is currently working on deploying 5G sites. The strategy is to prioritize investment in high-value mobile and convergent customers in urban centers.
  • Network Expansion: Telecom Argentina plans to continue expanding its FTTH network, migrating xDSL connections to FTTH, and upgrading HFC networks to enhance performance and coverage.

  • Mobile Services: The company is transitioning from 4G to 5G assets based on consumer demand.
  • Broadband Segment: With over 65% coverage of homes in the country and an average network speed exceeding 100 megabytes per second, Telecom Argentina is actively deploying fiber in areas with xDSL connections and upgrading its HFC network for improved performance.
