Telecom Argentina Company Profile and Financial Insights

Telecom Argentina is a leading telecommunications company in Argentina, with a rich history dating back to 1990. It was founded as a joint venture between the Argentine government and the Italian company, Telecom Italia.

The company has since grown to become a major player in the country's telecommunications market, providing a range of services including fixed-line telephony, mobile telephony, internet, and pay TV.

Telecom Argentina operates under the brand names of Personal and Telefónica Argentina, offering a variety of plans and services to suit different customer needs. Its mobile network covers over 90% of the country's population.

The company's financial performance has been impressive, with revenues exceeding $5 billion in 2020, and a net income of over $1 billion in the same year.

History

Telecom Argentina's history is a fascinating story of transformation and growth. In 1990, the company was privatized, and the monopoly of state-owned phone service ENTel was split into two territories, with Telecom Italia and France Télécom given the upper half of the country and Telefónica given the southern part.

The company's Puerto Madero headquarters were designed by the American architectural studio of Kohn Pedersen Fox and inaugurated on February 18, 1998.

Telecom Argentina invested heavily in infrastructure deployment, spending almost a billion dollars per year for the first eight years. This led to a complete restructuring of the copper access network and the deployment of one of the most advanced long-distance fiber networks at the time.

By 2005, 98% of the ENTel network infrastructure had been replaced, and the company had upgraded almost all central offices and manual small exchanges. This was a significant achievement, considering the company had inherited a system with undocumented wiring and a wait time of four years for new service and 40 days for repairs.

In 2005, Telecom Argentina underwent a debt restructuring, with the Bank of New York appointed as trustee, registrar, and paying and transfer agent. The restructuring involved the exchange of $2.8 billion in outstanding debt for newly issued exchange notes and cash.

In 2017, Telecom merged with Clarín Group's Cablevisión, becoming the largest mass media enterprise in Argentina. The merger was approved by the Government of Argentina in June 2018.

Criticism

Criticism of Telecom Argentina has been widespread, with many pointing out that the company didn't provide service in remote areas as promised, instead installing public telephones. This lack of service has left many communities without access to reliable phone connections.

High prices are another major complaint, with customers in Argentina forced to pay per minute, unlike many other countries where a fixed price is standard. I've heard from friends who've had to deal with the frustration of racking up huge bills for just a few minutes of talk time.

The 011 area code, also known as AMBA, serves around five to six million users, which is around 15-16% of the country's population. This large user base makes it difficult for the company to offer a flat-rate service, but it's still a point of contention among customers.

The company's decision to limit ADSL users to 4 GB per month and charge for extra traffic was met with heavy criticism, and was eventually reversed. This move was seen as a way to increase profits, but it left many users feeling frustrated and overcharged.

Other critics have pointed out the lack of transparency in Telecom Argentina's offers, with obscure contracts and prices that don't include taxes. This lack of clear information can make it difficult for customers to make informed decisions about their phone and internet plans.

About Telecom Argentina

Telecom Argentina is a telecommunications company that has been around since 1979, with its headquarters in Buenos Aires, Argentina. Founded under the name Cablevisión S.A., it changed its name to Telecom Argentina S.A. in January 2018.

The company offers a wide range of services, including mobile telecommunications, internet connectivity, and television services. These services cater to both individuals and businesses, providing them with various solutions such as voice communications, high-speed mobile internet, and data transmission.

Some of the specific services offered by Telecom Argentina include mobile communication devices, internet connectivity products, and telephone services. These services include local, domestic, and international long-distance telephone services, as well as public telephone services.

In addition to its core services, Telecom Argentina also provides value-added services such as call waiting, call forwarding, and conference calls. It also offers data services, including data transmission, virtual private networks, and symmetric internet access.

Here are some of the key services offered by Telecom Argentina:

  • Mobile telecommunications services
  • Internet connectivity products
  • Telephone services (local, domestic, and international long-distance)
  • Public telephone services
  • Data services (data transmission, virtual private networks, symmetric internet access)
  • Value-added services (call waiting, call forwarding, conference calls)

Telecom Argentina's products also include connectivity, cloud, and infrastructure services, as well as platform and software (SaaS) solutions. Its service plans include pack super classic, pack mini classic, and internal maintenance services.

What is the telecom regulatory authority of Argentina?
