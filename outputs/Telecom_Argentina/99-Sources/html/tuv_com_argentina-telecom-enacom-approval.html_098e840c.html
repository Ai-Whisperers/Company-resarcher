Argentina - Telecom/ENACOM Approval

Ensure fast, mandatory ENACOM approval for telecom and wireless products to be sold in Argentina with our assistance

ENACOM (Ente Nacional De Comunicaciones), the national body of communications in Argentina, regulates that all products that connect to the public switched communications network and products emitting radio frequency require ENACOM approval and registration before they can be commercialized in the country. TÜV Rheinland is recognized by the Argentine government to evaluate technical requirements and provide direct product testing services for telecom and wireless products to be sold in Argentina, whether manufactured locally or imported. Ask how we can help you efficiently obtain approval for your telecommunications and wireless products.
