Scope
Total telecom and pay-TV service revenue in Argentina will grow at a 0.9% CAGR between 2024-2029, driven by fixed and mobile broadband services. Mobile broadband will be the largest revenue contributor to  total telecom service revenue in Argentina over the forecast period, growing from $3.2 billion in 2024 to $4.3 billion in 2029 at a 6.3% CAGR. Key growth factors include growth in mobile internet subscriptions, higher data usage, and 5G adoption.
Key Highlights
Fixed broadband service revenue will also grow from $2.0 billion in 2024 to $2.2 billion in 2029 at a 2.5% CAGR, supported by subscription growth, demand for high-speed broadband over and growth of fiber-to-the-home/business (FTTH/B) accesses.
