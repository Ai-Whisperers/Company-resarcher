Argentina’s digital landscape is undergoing a seismic shift, driven by Telecom Argentina’s ambitious expansion of its 5G network and a significant investment in data center infrastructure. As the company races towards a goal of 750 5G sites by year-end, it’s not just about faster mobile speeds; it’s about laying the foundation for a new era of enterprise innovation, particularly in the burgeoning field of artificial intelligence.
The carrier’s aggressive 5G rollout, currently standing at 550 active sites and targeting 750 by the close of 2024, signals a clear acceleration in network deployment across the nation. This expansion is more than just about increasing subscriber connectivity; it’s a strategic move to empower businesses with the ultra-low latency and high bandwidth necessary for next-generation applications. Telecom Argentina is already demonstrating this commitment by introducing private 5G solutions, leveraging network slicing capabilities on its recently deployed core network in Buenos Aires. These private networks are already making inroads, with approximately 25 deployments reported across vital sectors like mining, oil, ports, and agriculture.
This push into private 5G is a critical differentiator, offering businesses dedicated, secure, and high-performance networks tailored to their specific operational needs.
