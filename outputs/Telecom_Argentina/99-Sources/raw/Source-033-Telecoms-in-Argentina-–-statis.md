Argentina
From the magnificent Iguazú Falls to the glaciers of Patagonia, Argentina spans a vast territory and a population of almost 50 million who, like elsewhere in the world, are hungry for data, fast internet connection, and reliable telecommunications. Like other countries in Latin America and beyond, the telecoms industry in Argentina is characterized by
rising mobile telephony revenues
, estimated at nearly 1.5 billion U.S. dollars in 2023, representing almost six times the
amount generated by fixed-line services
in the same period.
