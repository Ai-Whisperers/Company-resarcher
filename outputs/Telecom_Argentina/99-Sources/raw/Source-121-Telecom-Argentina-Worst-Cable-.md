History of Service Degradation
The history of service degradation by Telecom Argentina can be traced back to its early days when it was known as ENTel. ENTel was the state-owned phone service provider in Argentina until its privatization in 1990. After privatization, Telecom Argentina took over the telecommunications market in the northern part of the country, including Buenos Aires.
However, even after privatization, Telecom Argentina struggled to provide reliable and high-quality services to its customers. The company faced numerous technical and operational challenges, resulting in service degradation and customer dissatisfaction.
