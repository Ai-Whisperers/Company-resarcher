Telefónica Argentina
is a telecommunications company, a subsidiary of
Telecom Argentina
, founded in 1990 after the privatization of the National Telecommunications Company (ENTel). It is currently one of the two largest telephone and Internet service operators in the country, along with its main competitor (until 2025) and current parent company, Telecom Argentina.
