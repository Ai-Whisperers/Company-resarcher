Telecom Argentina S.A. is one of Argentina's leading telecommunications companies, offering a wide spectrum of services including fixed-line and mobile telephony (Personal), broadband internet access (Fibertel), pay television (Flow), and integrated ICT solutions for corporate clients. Following its merger with Cablevisión, Telecom Argentina has solidified its position as a convergent communications provider, heavily investing in network infrastructure, 5G development, and digital transformation services. The company also has significant operations in Paraguay through Nucleo S.A. (Personal) and in Uruguay (Personal Uruguay), extending its reach and services in the region.
