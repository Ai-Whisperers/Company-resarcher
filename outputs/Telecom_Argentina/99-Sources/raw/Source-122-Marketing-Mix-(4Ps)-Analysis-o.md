About 4Ps Model

	2017). The model is commonly referred to as the marketing mix. The marketing mix of the Telecom Argentina S A allows and
	facilitates it in achieving its marketing objectives as well as in positively influencing the target audience (Baines,
	Fill, & Rosengren, 2017). The elements identified in the marketing mix are typically used by the Telecom Argentina S A for
	marketing its product and service, and for brand development and building activities. These elements are critically
	fundamental for the development and creation of marketing plans and marketing strategies by the Telecom Argentina S A –
	especially for developing and sustaining competitive advantage (Chernev, 2018; Stead & Hastings, 2018; Grewal
	& Levy, 2021). Telecom Argentina S A ensures that the elements identified for the marketing mix model work together
	cohesively, and complement each other in all its marketing strategies and plans (Abratt & Bendixen, 2018; Deepak
	& Jeyakumar, 2019).
