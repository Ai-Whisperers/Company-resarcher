Telecom Argentina S.A., incorporated in 1990 and headquartered at General Hornos 690, Buenos Aires, Argentina, is one of the country’s largest and most diversified telecommunications and media companies. It provides quadruple-play services—including mobile and fixed telephony, broadband internet, cable television, and digital financial services—to over 30 million customers across Argentina, Paraguay, and Uruguay. The company operates under several leading brands: Personal (mobile and internet), Flow (TV and streaming), and Personal Pay (digital wallet). Telecom Argentina was originally formed during the privatization of Argentina’s state-owned telecom provider ENTel, and began operations in the northern region of the country. A major milestone occurred in 2018, when it merged with Cablevisión S.A., consolidating its position as a convergent telecom and media leader. The company is publicly traded on the Buenos Aires Stock Exchange (TECO2) and the New York Stock Exchange (TEO). Its main shareholders include Cablevisión Holding S.A., Fintech Advisory Inc., and Grupo Clarín, with Roberto Daniel Nóbile serving as CEO.
