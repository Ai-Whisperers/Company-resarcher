Telecom Argentina's $2 Trillion Financial Swing Clouds Post-Acquisition Growth

Summary
Telecom Argentina's financial landscape underwent a dramatic transformation in the first half of 2025, largely driven by its substantial US$1.245 billion acquisition of Telefónica Móviles Argentina (TMA). While this move significantly bolstered the company's scale, propelling its Adjusted EBITDA up 46% to $1.007 trillion, the integration faces considerable regulatory headwinds and macroeconomic volatility continues to impact reported results.
The TMA acquisition, finalized on February 24, 2025, made 1H25 results incomparable to prior periods. TMA alone contributed $968.8 billion in revenue and $226.8 billion in Adjusted EBITDA from late February through June 30. This strategic expansion solidifies Telecom Argentina's position in the converged ICT services market, promising enhanced network coverage and speeds. Despite the headline growth, the Argentine Antitrust Commission (CNDC) issued a preliminary objection regarding the TMA acquisition, prohibiting direct integration or consolidation of businesses. This provisional measure impedes the realization of expected synergies and cost savings, forcing the company to operate two major entities independently and introducing significant uncertainty.
Adding complexity is Argentina's hyperinflationary environment, where financial statements are restated to current pesos. The National CPI surged 39.4% year-over-year, while the US$/$ exchange rate devalued 32.1% year-over-year. This dynamic profoundly impacted financial results, as the company swung to a net financial loss of $215 billion in 1H25, a stark reversal from a $1.79 trillion gain in 1H24. This $2.01 trillion negative shift was primarily due to foreign currency exchange losses on borrowings, as inflation outpaced currency devaluation.
Beneath the inflationary and acquisition-driven growth, core operational metrics reveal challenges. Mobile Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) in Paraguay, when measured in constant pesos, decreased 34.4%, failing to keep pace with inflation and suggesting real price erosion. Furthermore, Telecom Argentina's mobile customer base in Argentina (excluding TMA) declined 1.3%, and fixed telephony customers decreased 1.0%. This indicates underlying pressure on pricing power and subscriber retention.
On the financing front, total borrowings increased to $4.416 trillion from $3.312 trillion at year-end 2024. However, Telecom Argentina demonstrated proactive debt management by issuing US$1 billion in new notes and prepaying US$784 million of the TMA acquisition loans. This strategic refinancing, even amidst economic volatility, signals continued access to capital markets. Concurrently, the company's digital platforms, like "Personal Pay," saw robust growth, increasing their user base by 41% to 4.2 million, diversifying revenue streams beyond traditional connectivity.
Investors will closely monitor the CNDC's ongoing review and Telecom Argentina's ability to navigate the complex regulatory and macroeconomic landscape to realize the full value of the TMA acquisition and address underlying operational weaknesses.
