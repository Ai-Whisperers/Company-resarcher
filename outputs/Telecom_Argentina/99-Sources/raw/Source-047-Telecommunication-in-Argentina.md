Mobile communications and Internet in Argentina
Compared to the
United States
, Argentina is at the forefront of the development of telecommunications. Under the country code +54, there were a total of 70.50 million connections in 2024. Among them were 64.08 million mobile phones, which corresponds to an average of 1.4 per person. In the US, this figure is 1.1 mobile phones per person.

Internet speed for cell phones and fixed network
With an average download speed of 105.42 Mbit/second for fixed-network broadband internet, Argentina ranks 56th in an international comparison. The upload rate was only 57.22 Mbit/second (60th place).
In
mobile internet
, i.e., on tablets and smartphones, Argentina comes 67th with a download speed of 59.72 Mbit/second. The upload speed of around 11 Mbit was only enough for 84th place.
The
Speedtest Global Index
published regularly by Ookla is based on several million individual measurements in October 2025 from 156 countries.
Mobile phone rates in Argentina
Every year, the
International Telecommunication Union
calculates for many countries what a mobile phone contract costs to use. A monthly budget averaging
US$ 9.50
was calculated Argentina in 2025. Compared to the previous year, this amount has fallen by 32 percent.
The ITU refers to the costs for monthly use and includes 140 telephony minutes, 20 text messages and a data transfer of 5 GB. The transfer speed should be the minimum of a 3G connection, i.e. at least UMTS, which is already outdated in many parts of the world. 5 GBytes doesn't sound like much to us. However, in a global comparison, this is already a very high consumption. If you don't take a fixed amount in US dollars, but convert it to economic output per inhabitant, a user in Argentina spends 0.85% of their income on mobile telephony. Worldwide, this rate is around 4.7 percent.
In the
USA
, the monthly cost was around $47.40.
According to the OECD, total costs in the telecommunications sector (i.e. not just limited to mobile communications) rose by 35.7% between October 2024 and October 2025.

Broadband internet in Argentina 2001 - 2024
Argentina is lagging behind in the expansion of broadband internet connections. Around 90 percent of all residents have access to the internet. There are around twelve million Internet connections nationwide that are at least faster than the former ISDN (more than 256 kbit/s). All fixed connections via DSL, cable or satellite were counted here, but not those connected to the internet via mobile communications. This would be around 26 subscriptions per hundred inhabitants, although multiple use already occurs because there is usually more than one person living in a household and people usually use another Internet connection at work.

Mobile phones 1990 - 2024
The increasing prevalence of cell phones and the simultaneous decline in landline phones clearly indicate a change in Argentina. While the number of landlines has fallen significantly in recent years, the spread of cell phones has risen to 140 cell phone subscriptions per 100 people at last count. Such a clear shift does not occur in all countries, but the trend points in the same direction internationally.
