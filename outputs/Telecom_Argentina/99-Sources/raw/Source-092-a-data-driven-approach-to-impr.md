Introduction
Client churn is a major problem for telecom companies as it reduces profit. In a saturated market, retaining customers is challenging and less costly than acquiring new ones. Previous studies on customer churn exist, but this study aims to predict telecom client churn through discriminant analysis and logistic regression using data from three major Chinese telecom companies. It also proposes research questions regarding factors leading to customer loss and how to predict it.
Literature Review
Technological progress has changed the telecom industry's competition. Various factors like tariffs, service quality, and sociodemographic characteristics influence customer churn. Data mining is an efficient way to predict customer churn, and different methods like decision trees and logistic regression have been used, each with their own advantages and disadvantages. Machine learning and deep learning are also suitable for prediction.
Hypotheses and Proposed Model
Hypotheses were proposed regarding the impact of various fees (such as monthly, roaming, network fees) and SMS quantities on customer loss. Empirical analysis was used to evaluate different data mining methods for predicting customer churn. Different telecom scenarios in Taiwan, SyriaTel, and India were considered for hypothesis assessment.
Methodology
Client data from three major Chinese telecom operators (China Mobile, China Unicom, and China Telecom) for 4,126 clients from 2007 - 2018 were used. SPSS was employed for data analysis, and methods like factor analysis, Pearson correlation, chi - square, discriminant, and logistic regression analysis were used to predict customer churn.
Factor Analysis to Characterize Expense, Call, and SMS Attributes
Factor analysis was conducted on expense, call, and SMS data. For expense data, KMO and Bartlett tests showed data were suitable for factor analysis, and common factors F1 and F2 were found to characterize expense attributes. Similar factor analyses were done for call data (resulting in factors F3 and F4) and SMS data (resulting in factors F5 and F6).
Discriminant Telecom Customer Loss Model
A discriminant model was established. Eigenvalues, Wilks’ lambda discriminant test, Fisher’s linear discriminant function, and an accuracy test were carried out. The discriminant function had an eigenvalue of 0.030 and a canonical correlation of 0.171. The first discriminant function explained 97.1% of variations. The overall prediction accuracy rate was 75%.
Logistic Regression Model of Telecom Customer Churn Prediction
A logistic regression model was built with 19 independent variables. Hypothesis tests were conducted. Some factors had a significant influence on customer churn, while others did not. The model had an overall prediction accuracy of 93.94% and good fit, showing that expense, SMS, and call information factors influence customer churn.

Abstract
Numerous valuable clients can be lost to competitors in the telecommunication industry, leading to profit loss. Thus, understanding the reasons for client churn is vital for telecommunication companies. This study aimed to develop a churn prediction model to predict telecom client churn through customer segmentation. Data were collected from three major Chinese telecom companies, and Fisher discriminant equations and logistic regression analysis were used to build a telecom customer churn prediction model. According to the results, it can be concluded that the telecom customer churn model constructed by regression analysis had higher prediction accuracy (93.94%) and better results.
