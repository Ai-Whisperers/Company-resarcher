Argentina: ENACOM Updates CABFRA Framework and Opens New Satellite Frequency Bands
Argentina is advancing its satellite communications landscape! On
October 23rd, 2025
, the
National Communications Entity (ENACOM)
issued a
new Summary Resolution
that updates the
Argentine Frequency Attribution Chart (CABFRA)
and introduces a major adjustment to national spectrum policy. The resolution allocates the
17.3–17.7 GHz frequency band
to the
Fixed Satellite Service (FSS)
in the
space-to-Earth (downlink)
direction, with
primary status
.
What does this mean?
This regulatory update designates the 17.3–17.7 GHz portion of the spectrum for
downlink satellite operations
, strengthening Argentina’s alignment with
international telecommunications standards
and ensuring efficient use of its national frequency resources.
Under the new framework,
service protection measures, operational conditions, and frequency-sharing parameters
must adhere to a series of
specific National Notes
, consistent with the
International Telecommunication Union (ITU) Radio Regulations
.
Who will be impacted?
The resolution directly affects a range of key industry stakeholders, including:
GEO and NGSO Fixed Satellite Service (FSS) operators
, who can now leverage new downlink opportunities.
Broadcasting Satellite Service (BSS) feeder link providers and license holders
, who must coordinate frequency use accordingly.
Satellite integrators and ISPs
, expanding broadband and connectivity solutions.
Earth station equipment manufacturers
, adjusting to updated spectrum parameters.
Why is this important?
The update represents a
strategic enhancement
to Argentina’s national frequency management framework. By harmonizing with ITU regulations, ENACOM ensures
legal certainty and international compatibility
for FSS operations—facilitating greater investment, technological integration, and spectrum efficiency.
In summary
ENACOM’s decision to incorporate the
17.3–17.7 GHz band
into the FSS domain marks a significant regulatory milestone for Argentina’s satellite sector. It strengthens operational clarity, fosters innovation, and positions the country as a forward-looking player in the global satellite communications arena—
balancing technical precision with policy coherence
.
