So, how much do SIM cards cost in Argentina?
You can get an Argentine
SIM card

What is the Best SIM Card in Argentina?
If you want to buy
the best SIM card
in Argentina, I recommend going with Personal. It has the best coverage and the fastest speeds. Moreover, it is also the only mobile operator with a 5G NR network (also for prepaid). Consider Claro for more generous data plans (Gigantes & Tourist SIM card).
So, who are all these mobile operators and what options are available for you?
Argentina has three mobile operators offering prepaid services that are
easily accessible to travelers
:
Claro Argentina
Movistar Argentina
Personal Argentina
Anyone 18 years or older (previously 16) living in or visiting Argentina can buy an Argentine SIM card without restrictions.

Where Can You Buy an Argentine SIM Card (+Prices)?
You can get an Argentine SIM card in Claro, Movistar & Personal stores. Resellers, like kiosks, also sell SIM cards. Some
Argentine
airports sell (tourist) SIM cards (at inflated prices).
You can expect to pay the following for Argentine SIM cards:
Claro Argentina
: free from official Claro stores, else 50 ARS from resellers
Movistar Argentina
: free from official Movistar stores, else 50 ARS from resellers
Personal Argentina
: free from official Personal stores, else 50 ARS from resellers
The following Argentine mobile operators also have SIM cards targeting tourists:
Claro Argentina
: 2500 ARS
Personal Argentina
: 800 ARS
Note that Argentine SIM cards can be more expensive than those listed above when purchased at an airport.
