## Growthsignals

### Item
- **Date:** September 9, 2025
- **Company:** Telecom Argentina
- **Signal:** 5G Expansion
- **Details:** Telecom Argentina aims to have 750 active 5G sites by year-end, up from 550. They are introducing private 5G solutions via network slicing in Buenos Aires.
- **Source:** Multiple Sources

### Item
- **Date:** September 9, 2025
- **Company:** Telecom Argentina
- **Signal:** Data Center Upgrades
- **Details:** The company is upgrading 16 data centers to 10MW capacity to meet AI and enterprise demand, with a flagship hyperscale facility in Pacheco. Expansion depends on demand and contract signings.
- **Source:** Multiple Sources

### Item
- **Date:** Mid-2025
- **Company:** Telecom Argentina
- **Signal:** Capital Expenditure Increase
- **Details:** Capital expenditure surged 53.7% to ARS 481.2 billion ($355M) in mid-2025, driven by Telefónica acquisition and infrastructure investments.
- **Source:** Multiple Sources

### Item
- **Date:** September 8, 2025
- **Company:** Telecom Argentina
- **Signal:** Private 5G Networks
- **Details:** Enacom's spectrum policy enables private 5G networks, supporting 25 existing deployments in mining, oil, and agriculture sectors.
- **Source:** Multiple Sources

### Item
- **Date:** September 8, 2025
- **Company:** Telecom Argentina
- **Signal:** Enterprise Growth
- **Details:** Telecom prioritizes enterprise growth through 5G and data infrastructure to strengthen market competitiveness, despite economic challenges. Enterprise unit is growing at double digits.
- **Source:** Multiple Sources

### Item
- **Date:** Early 2025
- **Company:** Telecom Argentina
- **Signal:** Telefónica Móviles Argentina (TMA) Acquisition
- **Details:** Acquisition of Telefónica Móviles Argentina (TMA) added 2.5 million Paraguayan subscribers and 3.2 million pay TV users.
- **Source:** Multiple Sources

### Item
- **Date:** November 10, 2025
- **Company:** Telecom Argentina
- **Signal:** Revenue Growth
- **Details:** Consolidated revenues reached P$5,622,561 million in 9M25. Service revenues totaled P$5,327,305 million in 9M25.
- **Source:** Telecom Argentina S.A. Announces Consolidated Results for The Nine-Month Period ("9M25") and Third Quarter of Fiscal Year 2025 ("3Q25")

### Item
- **Date:** November 10, 2025
- **Company:** Telecom Argentina
- **Signal:** Customer Base Evolution
- **Details:** Pay TV subscribers increased to 3.2 million (+1.4% vs. 9M24). Fixed broadband accesses grew by 2.5%, totaling 4.1 million (+102 thousand vs. 9M24).
- **Source:** Telecom Argentina S.A. Announces Consolidated Results for The Nine-Month Period ("9M25") and Third Quarter of Fiscal Year 2025 ("3Q25")

### Item

### Item
- **Date:** May 2, 2024
- **Company:** Telecom Argentina
- **Signal:** Joint Venture
- **Details:** Telecom Argentina and Intraway Announce Joint Venture to Drive Open Gateway Adoption. The new technology company will support mobile operators in the end-to-end deployment of the GSMA Open Gateway initiative to drive new revenues by exposing their networks through standard APIs.
