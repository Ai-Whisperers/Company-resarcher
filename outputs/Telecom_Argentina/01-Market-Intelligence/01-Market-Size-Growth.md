## Key Growth Drivers

### Mobile Broadband Adoption

Extensive LTE coverage and high mobile adoption rates are driving mobile broadband as the preferred method for internet access in Argentina. The mobile subscription penetration reached 134.1% of the population.

**Impact:** High### 5G Deployment

The launch of the first 5G service in February 2021, using re-farmed LTE frequencies, and planned 5G spectrum auctions are expected to further increase mobile broadband adoption. The government planned a 5G spectrum auction for February 2023 to increase government revenue and combat inflation.

**Impact:** Medium### Government Initiatives

Government initiatives such as the 'Plan Conectar', launched in September 2020, aim to improve internet access across the country, providing funds for coverage improvement initiatives.

**Impact:** Medium

## Emerging Trends

### Deregulation

Argentina's telecommunications industry has undergone significant transformations since the privatization in the 1990s, with a recent deregulation process starting in December 2023. This aims to attract investments, foster competition, and ensure more equitable access to technological services.

**Relevance to Company:** This deregulation could create opportunities for Telecom Argentina to expand its services and attract new investments.### Increasing Broadband Penetration

Fixed broadband revenue is expected to increase, driven by higher broadband penetration and rising average revenue per user (ARPU).

**Relevance to Company:** Telecom Argentina can capitalize on this trend by expanding its fixed broadband infrastructure and service offerings.### 5G Adoption

Argentina launched its first 5G service in February 2021 using re-farmed LTE frequencies. A 5G spectrum auction was planned for February 2023. The adoption of the mobile broadband service is expected to increase further due to the upcoming 5G spectrum auctions.

**Relevance to Company:** Telecom Argentina needs to invest in 5G infrastructure and services to stay competitive and meet the increasing demand for high-speed internet.
## Regional Insights

Argentina's telecommunications market is characterized by high mobile penetration (134.1%). The market is undergoing deregulation to attract investments and foster competition. Mobile broadband is the preferred method for accessing the internet, driven by extensive LTE coverage and high mobile adoption rates. The government is actively working to improve internet access across the country through initiatives like the 'Plan Conectar'.

## Competitive Position

The Argentina telecom market is highly concentrated. Telefónica sold its Argentinian subsidiary to Telecom Argentina for $1.245 billion USD. Telecom Argentina acquired Telefónica Móviles Argentina, increasing its market presence. Key competitors include Telefónica and Motorola VICOM S.A. (MOVICOM).
