### History

Telecom Argentina was formed after the privatization of ENTel in 1990, initially as Sociedad Licenciataria Norte S.A. It operated as part of a duopoly with Telefónica de Argentina. Over time, it expanded its services to include mobile (Personal), cable modem (Arnet-Fibertel, now Flow), and cable operations (Cablevisión, now Flow). In 2025, Telecom Argentina acquired Telefónica Argentina.

## Unique Selling Proposition (USP)

Comprehensive telecommunications provider offering fixed-line, mobile, broadband, and pay-TV services, now further strengthened by the acquisition of Telefónica Argentina. Example: Offering bundled services and expanding broadband coverage with fiber optics and 5G technology.

## Value Proposition

Provides connectivity and communication solutions to individuals and businesses, offering a range of services from basic telephone to advanced digital services. Benefits include reliable network infrastructure, a wide range of services, and a focus on innovation and digital transformation.

## Brand Archetype

**Archetype:** Innovator

Telecom Argentina embodies the Innovator archetype through its commitment to technological advancement and its pioneering role in deploying new technologies like 5G. The company's focus on digital transformation and its investments in fiber optics demonstrate a desire to push boundaries and create cutting-edge solutions for its customers. The brand's efforts to expand its presence in multiple countries also align with the Innovator's desire to explore new territories and opportunities.

## Positioning Statement

> Telecom Argentina is the leading telecommunications provider in Argentina, offering a comprehensive suite of services and driving digital transformation through innovation and reliable connectivity.

## Brand Strengths

### Comprehensive Service Portfolio

Offers a wide range of telecommunications services, including mobile, broadband, pay TV, and fixed-line, allowing it to cater to diverse customer needs.

**Competitive Advantage:** Reduces customer churn by offering bundled services and creates cross-selling opportunities.
### Technological Advancement

Invests in deploying fiber optics and 5G technology, positioning itself as a leader in innovation.

**Competitive Advantage:** Provides faster and more reliable services, attracting customers seeking advanced technology.
### Market Position

Significant customer base and expanded market share due to the acquisition of Telefónica Argentina.

**Competitive Advantage:** Increased market power and economies of scale.
### Award-Winning Mobile Network

Personal, a Telecom Argentina brand, was recognized by Ookla for having the best mobile network and fastest 5G in Argentina in 2025.

**Competitive Advantage:** Enhances brand reputation and attracts customers seeking high-quality mobile services.

## Brand Challenges

### Regulatory Scrutiny

The acquisition of Telefónica Argentina faced scrutiny from regulatory bodies due to concerns about market concentration.

**Impact:** Potential delays in the integration process and limitations on market power.
### Market Concentration Concerns

Increased market share resulting from the acquisition of Telefónica Argentina raises concerns about potential monopolistic practices.

**Impact:** May lead to regulatory intervention and limitations on pricing and service offerings.

## Core Values

### Innovation

### Reliability

Focus on providing stable and high-quality network services.
### Customer Focus

Providing self-service portals to manage customer needs and improve customer experience.

## Marketing Campaigns

### Expansion of 5G Network

Telecom Argentina deployed 188 new 5G sites during 2024, reaching a total of 266 in 71 cities, with plans to add 500 more sites in 2025.

**Results:** Enhanced network coverage and faster mobile internet speeds, leading to improved customer satisfaction.
