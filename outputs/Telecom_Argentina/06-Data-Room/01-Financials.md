## Investments & Capital Expenditures

Capex increased 53.7% year-on-year to $355 million as of June 30, 2025. Telecom Argentina aims to end 2025 with 750 5G sites. All 16 of Telecom Argentina's data centers will be expanded to 10MW capacity.

## Debt Position

Consolidated Net Financial Debt increased 44.3% in constant currency to P$4.4 trillion since December 31, 2024. Telecom Argentina issued a new international bond due in 2033, raising $1 billion to refinance acquisition loans.
