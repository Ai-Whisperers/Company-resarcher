## History

- **2000-2002:** Operated as Ñe'e de Copesa S.A. (Comunicaciones Personales S.A.) offering fixed telephony using GSM technology in the PCS band (1900).
- **2002:** Brand name changed to Porthable, launching GSM mobile telephony service.
- **2005:** Hutchison Telecom sold operations to América Móvil; company name became AMX Paraguay S.A., brand name became CTI Móvil.
- **2007:** CTI Móvil launched 3G mobile service.
- **2008:** CTI Móvil rebranded as Claro.
- **2011:** Launched Claro TV, a digital satellite television (DTH) service.
- **2016:** Launched 4G-LTE service using AWS band 4 (1700/2100).
