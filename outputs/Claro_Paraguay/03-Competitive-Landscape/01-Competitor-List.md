## Indirect Competitors / Alternatives


### Starlink

**Type:** Satellite Internet Provider

Starlink provides satellite-based internet services in Paraguay, targeting rural and underserved areas. They offer an alternative to traditional fixed broadband.

**Potential Impact:** Can capture market share in areas where Claro's fixed broadband infrastructure is limited. Increased competition in rural areas. Starlink traffic surged 900x in 2024 after launch.


### Vox

**Type:** MVNO

A smaller mobile virtual network operator (MVNO) in Paraguay, operating on Tigo's network infrastructure.

**Potential Impact:** Offers cheaper prepaid plans, attracting price-sensitive customers. Limited coverage compared to major operators.


### Copaco

**Type:** MVNO

A smaller mobile virtual network operator (MVNO) in Paraguay, renting network access from Personal.

**Potential Impact:** Offers cheaper prepaid plans, attracting price-sensitive customers. Limited coverage compared to major operators.


## Emerging Threats


### Increased competition from MVNOs

MVNOs offering cheaper prepaid plans could attract price-sensitive customers, eroding Claro's market share.

**Timeline:** Ongoing

**Mitigation:** Offer competitive prepaid plans, focus on value-added services, and improve customer experience.


### Technological Disruption

Rapid advancements in technology (e.g., new wireless standards, satellite internet) could disrupt the market and require significant investments to keep up.

**Timeline:** Ongoing

**Mitigation:** Invest in R&D, monitor emerging technologies, and be prepared to adapt business strategies quickly.


## Claro Paraguay's Competitive Advantages


### Extensive 4G coverage

Claro has a wide 4G LTE coverage across Paraguay, with a 98.5% availability score as of 2024, providing reliable mobile data services.

**Sustainability:** Moderately sustainable, requires continuous investment in network infrastructure and upgrades.


### Acquisition of 3.5GHz spectrum

Claro acquired 200MHz in the 3.5GHz band, positioning them well for 5G deployment.

**Sustainability:** Highly sustainable in the short-to-medium term, as spectrum is a limited resource. Requires timely and effective deployment of 5G infrastructure.


### Part of América Móvil

Being part of América Móvil, a large multinational telecommunications conglomerate, provides Claro Paraguay with financial strength, access to technology, and economies of scale.

**Sustainability:** Highly sustainable, as it is based on a long-term corporate structure and global resources.


## Market Dynamics

**Market Concentration:** Oligopoly, with Tigo, Personal, and Claro dominating the market.

**Price Competition:** High, especially in prepaid mobile services.

**Key Differentiation Factors:**
- Network coverage and quality
- Price
- Data speeds
- Customer service
- Bundled services (e.g., TV, broadband)
- 5G availability

**Barriers to Entry:**
- High capital expenditure for network infrastructure
- Spectrum licensing costs
- Established brand recognition of existing players
- Regulatory hurdles

## Competitive Positioning Summary

Claro Paraguay is the third-largest mobile operator in Paraguay with a strong 4G network and plans for 5G deployment. While they have a smaller market share compared to Tigo and Personal, their membership in the América Móvil group gives them significant resources. Key differentiators include their extensive 4G coverage and their recent acquisition of 3.5GHz spectrum for 5G. Their strategic outlook involves expanding their 5G network and competing on data speeds and bundled services. They face strong competition from Tigo and Personal, as well as emerging threats from MVNOs and Starlink.
