### History

Claro Paraguay is part of América Móvil, a large telecommunications conglomerate in Latin America. It is one of the three major mobile network operators in Paraguay, along with Tigo and Personal.

## Unique Selling Proposition (USP)

Claro Paraguay's USPs include its extensive roaming services, allowing customers to use their data plans and WhatsApp for free while traveling in certain regions (America, Europe, Asia). They offer eSIM technology, eliminating the need for a physical SIM card. Additionally, Claro provides bundled entertainment benefits such as Claro TV, Claro Música, and Claro Video, including Prime Video and Claro Video with Paramount+ in some plans. They also provide a self-managed chip activation process, offering free WhatsApp, free data, and unlimited calls. Claro drive is another offering bundled in some plans. A customer review states "Si hay una tranquilidad que Claro siempre me dio es el respaldo en Roaming, recomendadísimo." (The peace of mind that Claro has always given me is the support in Roaming, highly recommended.)

## Value Proposition

Claro's core value proposition is to provide seamless connectivity and entertainment solutions for its customers. This includes reliable mobile services, extensive roaming options, and access to a variety of entertainment content. They offer convenience through multiple payment channels and self-service options. The value proposition is centered around ease of use, affordability, and comprehensive service offerings.

## Brand Archetype

**Archetype:** The Connector

Claro aims to connect people through its telecommunications services, facilitating communication and access to information and entertainment. This is evident in their emphasis on roaming services, which allow customers to stay connected while traveling, and their bundled entertainment offerings. The self-activation process and multiple payment channels also reflect a focus on making their services easily accessible and convenient for all customers.

## Positioning Statement

> Claro Paraguay is the leading telecommunications provider that delivers seamless connectivity, extensive roaming options, and rich entertainment content, empowering customers to stay connected and entertained wherever they are.

## Brand Strengths

### Roaming Services

Claro offers comprehensive roaming services, allowing customers to use their data and WhatsApp for free in various regions. This is a significant advantage for frequent travelers.

**Competitive Advantage:** Provides a hassle-free travel experience compared to competitors with limited or costly roaming options. Customer reviews highlight the reliability of Claro's roaming service.
### Entertainment Bundling

Claro includes access to Claro TV, Claro Música, Claro Video (including Prime Video and Paramount+) in some plans, adding value for customers seeking entertainment.

**Competitive Advantage:** Differentiates Claro by offering a more comprehensive package of services beyond just connectivity. Makes their plans more attractive to customers who value entertainment.
### Convenient Self-Service Options

Claro allows customers to activate their prepaid chips themselves and offers multiple payment channels, including WhatsApp and the Mi Claro app.

**Competitive Advantage:** Enhances customer experience by providing easy and accessible ways to manage their accounts and services. Reduces the need for physical store visits.

## Brand Challenges

### Coverage Gaps

While generally good, some customer reviews mention coverage gaps in certain areas, particularly on routes.

**Impact:** May lead to customer dissatisfaction in areas with poor coverage, impacting their perception of reliability.
### Perception of Internet Speed

Some customers have requested faster internet speeds.

**Impact:** May make them less competitive against other providers that are perceived to have faster internet speeds.

## Brand Evolution

Claro has evolved from a basic telecommunications provider to a comprehensive provider of connectivity and entertainment services, focusing on convenience and customer experience. They have also adopted new technologies like eSIM to stay competitive.

## Strategic Recommendations

Address coverage gaps to improve reliability in all areas. Increase focus on promoting internet speed and reliability to compete more effectively. Continue to leverage roaming services and entertainment bundling as key differentiators. Enhance customer service and support to address customer complaints and improve overall satisfaction.
