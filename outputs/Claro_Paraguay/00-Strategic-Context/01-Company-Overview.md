## History

Claro Paraguay, initially known as Ñe'e de Copesa S.A. under Hutchison Telecom, rebranded to Porthable in 2002. In 2005, América Móvil acquired the company, renaming it AMX Paraguay S.A. and rebranding as CTI Móvil. In 2008, it became Claro, aligning with América Móvil's Latin American branding.

### Key Milestones



- **2007:** CTI Móvil launches 3G mobile service
  

- **2008:** CTI Móvil rebranded to Claro
  

- **2011:** Claro launches Claro TV, a digital satellite television service
  

- **2016:** Claro launches 4G-LTE service
  

- **2025:** Claro acquired a block of 200 MHz in the 3.5 MHz band to deploy 5G technology
