Values: What is Net Serviços de Comunicação Core Values Statement?

The core values of Net Serviços de Comunicação, now operating as Claro Brasil, guide its strategic decisions and define its company culture. These principles underpin its operations, fostering innovation, prioritizing customer needs, and driving market leadership. The company's commitment to operational excellence ensures sustained growth and profitability.

Innovation

Claro Brasil consistently invests in advanced technologies, such as 5G networks and Generative AI. By the end of 2024, the company reached 13.7 million 5G customers, holding a 34.2% market share.

Customer Centricity

The company focuses on enhancing customer experience and retention, adding 703,000 new mobile customers in Q3 2024, expanding its base to 85 million. This commitment is also seen in its 'Claro Digital Solutions Hub' for SMEs.

Leadership and Market Dominance

Claro Brasil maintains strong market positions, leading fixed broadband with a 19.8% market share and pay-TV with 45.7% market share in 2024. Its B2B arm, Embratel, reported significant growth in SD-WAN services (45.1%) in Q3 2024.

Operational Excellence

The company's financial performance reflects its operational efficiency, with a 13.2% EBITDA growth in Q3 2024, reaching R$5.4 billion. Net revenue also rose by 7.4% to R$12.34 billion in the same period.
