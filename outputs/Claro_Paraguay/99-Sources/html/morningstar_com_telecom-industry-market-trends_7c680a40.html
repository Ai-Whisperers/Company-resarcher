What are the key telecommunications industry trends in 2025?

In the midst of weak US wireless and broadband customer growth, Canadian telecom stocks have little room to promote growth, as depressed valuations stagnate. Slower US wireless customer additions, coupled with competitive pressures, have prompted carriers to ramp up promotions. Retailers like T-Mobile exceeded growth projections in recent years, but now face more measured growth expectations as the market matures. Additionally, broadband markets trend downwards, with fewer new customers and rising fixed-wireless competition.
