About Tectum Cash Token

TCT is the utility and governance token of the Tectum ecosystem. It complements the existing TET token by adding ecosystem utility, community governance, and access to rewards from SoftNote ePOS operations. TCT enables holders to participate in proposals, support ecosystem development, and contribute to the growth of Tectum’s PayFi products.
