Mi Claro Paraguay is a free Android application developed by Claro Paraguay. It falls under the category of Social & Communication and serves as a self-service channel for Claro customers.

With Mi Claro Paraguay, users can easily manage their Claro services and accounts. They can view the details of their plan, check their consumption and available balances, and download their invoices. The application also allows users to change their Wi-Fi password and offers various additional features.
