Claro Paraguay is a major mobile network provider in Paraguay, offering extensive coverage and a wide range of services. Here are some key points about Claro Paraguay:

  1. Coverage:

    • Claro Paraguay has extensive nationwide coverage, particularly in rural areas, with a score of 98.5% for 4G availability.
    • The network provides 2G, 3G, 4G, and 5G services, ensuring a robust and reliable connection across the country.

  2. Advantages:

    • Claro is known for its consistent speed and wide reach, making it a preferred choice for both urban and rural areas.
    • The network offers a variety of plans, including budget-friendly options, which can be appealing to different types of users.

  3. Disadvantages:

    • Claro might be more expensive compared to its competitors, Personal and Tigo.
    • While Claro has extensive coverage, it may not be as affordable as Personal for some users.

  4. eSIM Availability:

    • Claro Paraguay supports eSIM technology, allowing users to purchase and install virtual SIM cards without the need for physical SIM cards.

  5. Customer Experience:

    • Claro Paraguay is part of the América Móvil group, which operates in several countries across Latin America, including Argentina, Brazil, Chile, and more.

  6. Additional Services:

    • Claro Paraguay offers a range of additional services, including mobile internet, social media access, and the latest smartphones, making it a comprehensive mobile network solution.

Overall, Claro Paraguay is a significant player in the country's mobile market, known for its extensive coverage and reliable services.

Claro Paraguay uses the GSM 1900 MHz (PCS) frequency band. This band operates in the 1,850–1,910 MHz range for the uplink and the 1,930–1,990 MHz range for the downlink. It was deployed in 1995 to support the growing demand for mobile services in the United States.

LTE

Name Interface Description
B4 (1700/2100 MHz AWS 1) LTE

Claro Paraguay uses the LTE B4 (1700/2100 MHz AWS 1) frequency band. The deployment date for this band is not specified.
B28 (700 MHz APT) LTE

Claro Paraguay uses LTE Band 28 (700 MHz APT) for its 4G LTE network. This band operates between 703 MHz and 803 MHz, with the uplink ranging from 703 MHz to 748 MHz and the downlink ranging from 758 MHz to 803 MHz. The band supports channel bandwidths of 5, 10, 15, and 20 MHz. It is known for its excellent propagation characteristics, allowing signals to penetrate buildings and cover larger geographic areas with less infrastructure. Claro Paraguay deployed this band in October 2016.
