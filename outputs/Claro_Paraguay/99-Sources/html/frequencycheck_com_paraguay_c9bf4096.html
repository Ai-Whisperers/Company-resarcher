Paraguay's mobile network has experienced significant growth and development over the years. Historically, the country's mobile network has been driven by the increasing demand for telecommunications services. In the early 1990s, mobile phone subscriptions began to rise, with the number of mobile cellular subscriptions increasing from 0.1 per 100 people in 1992 to 128 per 100 people by 2022.

The deployment of 4G networks and a new fiber backbone has been a major driver of revenue growth in Paraguay's telecommunications market. This infrastructure expansion has improved mobile data services, contributing to the growth of mobile data revenue.

Tigo Paraguay is a leading mobile operator in the country, known for its focus on network expansion and modernization. Other major players include Personal Paraguay, Claro Paraguay, and VOX. These operators have played a crucial role in expanding mobile coverage and improving service quality across the country.

Despite the growth, Paraguay still lags behind other countries in terms of mobile internet speeds, ranking 123rd globally in download speed and 129th in upload speed as of April 2024. However, the country's mobile phone rates are relatively affordable, with an average monthly cost of $14.50 in 2022, which is significantly lower than in the United States.
