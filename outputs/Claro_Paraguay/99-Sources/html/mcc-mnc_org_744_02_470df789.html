AMX Paraguay S.A., operating under the Claro brand, is a major mobile network operator in Paraguay, South America. Identified by its Mobile Country Code (MCC) of 744 and Mobile Network Code (MNC) of 02, Claro Paraguay provides a wide range of telecommunications services, including mobile voice, data, and internet access, to a significant portion of the Paraguayan population. As a subsidiary of América Móvil, one of the largest telecommunications corporations in Latin America, Claro leverages significant resources and expertise to deliver its services. It competes with other networks in Paraguay, such as Personal and Tigo, vying for market share by offering various plans, coverage options, and technological advancements. Claro's presence in Paraguay plays a critical role in connecting communities and businesses across the country. The network's infrastructure supports a growing demand for mobile connectivity, driving economic growth and facilitating access to information. Like other mobile providers in South & Central America and worldwide, Claro is continually investing in upgrades and expansions to its network, aiming to improve coverage, increase data speeds, and introduce new technologies such as 4G LTE and potentially 5G in the future. Its ongoing efforts contribute to Paraguay's overall digital landscape and its integration into the global communications network.
