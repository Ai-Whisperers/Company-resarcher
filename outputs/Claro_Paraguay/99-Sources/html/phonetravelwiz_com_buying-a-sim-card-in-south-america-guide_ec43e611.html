Top-Up, Recharge & Balance Check in Uruguay

Antel and Movistar Uruguay top-up vouchers are sold in Antel stores (1/2) and VIVA Movistar stores, kiosks, gas stations & grocery stores.

To top up your Antel SIM card, dial *77, select option 1, and enter the VoucherCode.

For Movistar, use the Mi Movistar Uruguay (My Movistar Uruguay app – Android/iOS)

You can also tell the salesperson how much credit you want, your phone number, pay, and they will top up your SIM card with the POS system.

Recharging online with international debit and credit cards is possible for Antel and Movistar.

Checking your balance can be done by dialing *611 or texting SALDO to 226 with Antel. For Movistar, text SALDO to 222.
