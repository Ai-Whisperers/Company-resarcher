    Company Background

    Claro Paraguay is a subsidiary of América Móvil, one of the largest telecommunications companies in Latin America. Operating primarily in the Paraguayan market, Claro offers a wide range of services including mobile telephony, fixed-line telephony, broadband internet, and digital television. The company plays a significant role in Paraguay's telecommunications sector, competing with other major providers such as Tigo Paraguay and Personal Paraguay. Claro Paraguay has been investing in network infrastructure and technology upgrades, including the rollout of 5G services, to enhance its service offerings and maintain market leadership. Financially, the company reported revenues of approximately $727.3 million for the nine months ended July 31, 2025, with a net profit decline attributed to lower gross margins and seasonal revenue variations. Strategic investments in talent and content creation have increased operating expenses, reflecting a focus on long-term growth and customer experience enhancement. The company’s total assets stood at around $1.03 billion, with cash reserves decreasing due to investments in proprietary content assets.
