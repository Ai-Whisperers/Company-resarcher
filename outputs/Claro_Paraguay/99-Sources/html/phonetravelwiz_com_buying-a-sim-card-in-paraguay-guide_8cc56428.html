Paraguay – known for its cheeses, for having the world's largest water reserver & for being one of the nations where old-school duels are still allowed.

But besides these (interesting) facts, Paraguay is also a fun travel destination.

The Monday Falls is probably the country's most famous waterfall.

Ybycuí National Park has several trails, waterfalls & locations you can stim in.

And the Misión Jesuística de la Santísima Trinidad is the remains of an old mission.

Oh, do not forget about Palacio de López – the presidential office

If you hate water, Paraguay may not fully be for you, but there are many non-water related activities in the country.

You may have read that staying in Paraguay may cost you three to ten million Paraguayan Guaraní, which sounds expensive.

But besides these nonsensical high currency numbers, Paraguay is one of the cheaper travel destinations in South America.

Luckily, SIM cards are inexpensive in Paraguay too.

You can get a Paraguayan SIM card for 5000 PYG (0.65 USD) from Claro & 10 000 PYG (1.35 USD) from Personal. Tigo has two starter packs priced at 5000 PYG and 10 000 PYG. SIM cards are available at the official stores of the mobile operators and at Asunción/Lunque-Silvio Pettirossi International Airport (ASU).

Those prices are not too bad, right?

And mobile data costs are reasonable too for global standards, but a bit high for regional ones.

If you want to buy the best SIM card in Paraguay, I recommend going with Tigo or Claro. Tigo has the best coverage and, overall, the best network. Claro has the fastest speeds. However, its (4G/LTE) coverage is the weakest in the country.

Top-Up, Recharge & Balance Check – Tigo Paraguay

Tigo top-up vouchers are sold in Tigo stores, grocery stores, corner shops & electronics shops throughout Paraguay.

To top up your Tigo SIM card, dial *222# and follow the instructions.

You can also tell the salesperson how much credit you want, your phone number, pay, and they will top up your SIM card with the POS system.

Dial *162, *611# and select option 1, or text S to 162 to check your Tigo balance.

Note that you can check your balance with S to 162 for free twice a day. Each subsequent balance inquiry will cost you 50 PYG/check.

You can also top up and check your balance (for free) on the Mi Tigo Paraguay (My Tigo Paraguay) app (Android/iOS).
