2. Mobile Virtual Network Operators (MVNOs) in Paraguay
Smaller mobile virtual network operators (MVNOs) in Paraguay include:
Vox: Uses Tigo’s network infrastructure
Copaco: Rents network access from Personal
Núcleo: Another MVNO on Personal’s network
These smaller carriers rent the major networks to offer cheaper prepaid plans. However, they depend on the big operators for coverage meaning service quality varies.
II. How to Choose the Best Mobile Operator in Paraguay
Factors to consider when selecting Paraguay’s best mobile operator:
1. Network Coverage & Technology
Verify carrier offers strong coverage for areas where service is needed (work, home, travel)
Ensure the network provides sufficient 3G/4G speeds and capacity
2. Cost & Plan Types
Evaluate prepaid and postpaid plan rates
Assess charges for calls, texts, and mobile data
Consider roaming costs if traveling abroad
3. Customer Service Reputation
Check online reviews regarding customer support experiences
Consider the convenience of store locations for assistance
4. Value Added Services
Review if the operator offers useful extras like entertainment bundles, finance tools
Weighing needs across these areas results in the operator that offers the best fit at the fairest price.
