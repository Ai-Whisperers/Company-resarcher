## History

Claro Paraguay was established in 2000 and has undergone several transitions, including changes in ownership and branding, to become one of the leading telecommunications companies in Paraguay.

### Key Milestones



- **2002:** Rebranded to Porthable
  

- **2005:** Acquired by América Móvil
  

- **2007:** Launched 3G network
  

- **2008:** Rebranded to Claro
  

- **2011:** Launched Claro TV
  

- **2016:** Launched 4G LTE network
