## Emerging Trends

### 5G deployment

Conatel has granted Claro a license to operate in the 3.5 GHz band, enabling the deployment of 5G technology. Claro acquired a block of 200 MHz in the 3.5 MHz band in September 2025. A 2024–2025 auction was planned to reach 50% 5G coverage a year after launch.

**Relevance to Company:** Claro is positioned as a pioneer in the Paraguayan market, promising ultra-high-speed connectivity.### Fiber optic network expansion

The country is working on unifying fiber optic networks to improve connectivity, especially in remote areas. As of 2021, there was a national fiber backbone of about 18,000 km.

**Relevance to Company:** This expansion can improve Claro's service delivery and coverage, particularly in underserved regions.### Increased internet penetration

Paraguay had about 78% of its population online in 2025, with urban usage over 80% and rural usage around 64%.

**Relevance to Company:** This trend increases the potential customer base for Claro's internet services.
## Regional Insights

Paraguay had about 78% of its population online in 2025, below the Latin American average of about 85%. Urban usage is over 80%, while rural usage is around 64%. Rural connectivity remains challenging, with about 214,000 Paraguayans (over 3%) still relying on 3G or older networks. The country is a landlocked territory and needs agreements with service providers for international connectivity. In 2024, Paraguay obtained an investment grade rating from Moody's Ratings.

## Competitive Position

Claro has around 10–11% of the internet market share in Paraguay. Claro was the third-largest mobile operator in Paraguay as of 2019, with approximately 1.7 million subscribers, accounting for 23% of the market share. Tigo Paraguay leads the internet market with about 42% share, followed by Personal at about 26%. Claro acquired 200 MHz of spectrum in the 3.5 GHz band in 2025, positioning itself to lead the 5G revolution in Paraguay.
