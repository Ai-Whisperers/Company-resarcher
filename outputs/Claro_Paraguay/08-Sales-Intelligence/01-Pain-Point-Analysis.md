## Pain Points

### Item
- **Area:** Customer Service
- **Description:** Several reviews mention negative experiences with customer service, citing unhelpful or inefficient support.
- **Examples:** ['Customer service was unhelpful.', 'Customer service was average.', 'Representatives are not trained to resolve technical issues.']

### Item
- **Area:** Network Coverage/Reliability
- **Description:** Some users report below-average network coverage and instability, including frequent interruptions and signal drops.
- **Examples:** ['Network coverage is below average.', 'Signal drops frequently.', 'Inestabilidad de la conexión a Internet, las interrupciones frecuentes.']

### Item
- **Area:** Internet Service Quality
- **Description:** Complaints about internet service being poor, especially regarding speed and stability for online gaming.
- **Examples:** ["Inservible, si bien se dice que es 'LTE advanced' la señal se cae cada ves que quiero entrar a jugar algo en mi dispositivo.", 'Servicio de internet pésimo.']

### Item
- **Area:** Roaming Issues
- **Description:** Problems with roaming services in other countries, including lack of service despite requesting help.
- **Examples:** ['Servicio de roaming deficiente. No me dieron servicio fuera de chile, pese a que les solicite muchas veces ayuda.']

### Item
- **Area:** Pricing and Billing
- **Description:** Concerns about high prices not corresponding to the quality of service, and issues with billing efficiency.
- **Examples:** ['Precios elevados.', 'Para cobrar el mes de pago eficientes le llegan los cobros con 15 días anticipados pero si el usuario hace reclamos de nada.']

### Item
