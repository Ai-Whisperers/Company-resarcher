## Identified Pain Points

### Customer Churn and Acquisition Costs

The telecommunications market in Paraguay is competitive, with Claro competing against Tigo and Personal. High competition likely leads to customer churn as users switch providers for better deals or service. High CAC could be a result of this.

| Business Impact | Urgency |
|-----------------|---------|
| High churn directly impacts revenue and profitability. High CAC reduces profit margins on new customers. | High |
### Need for Enhanced Digital Customer Experience

While Claro offers 'Mi Claro' app and WhatsApp channel, customers may still prefer alternative channels. The need to drive adoption and improve user experience across digital platforms is ongoing.

| Business Impact | Urgency |
|-----------------|---------|
| Inefficient customer service channels can lead to higher operational costs and lower customer satisfaction. | Medium |
### Maintaining Competitive Pricing and Value

Claro needs to balance competitive pricing with profitability. Maintaining a compelling value proposition (data, minutes, speed) is crucial.

| Business Impact | Urgency |
|-----------------|---------|
| Failing to offer competitive pricing can lead to customer attrition. Reducing service quality to cut costs can also backfire. | High |

## Recommended Solutions

### Customer Experience Platform

**Addresses:** Need for Enhanced Digital Customer Experience

**Rationale:** A Customer Experience Platform can integrate all customer touchpoints (Mi Claro app, WhatsApp, online portal) to provide a unified view of the customer journey. This enables personalized interactions, proactive issue resolution, and improved self-service capabilities. It will help claro better understand customer behavior and preferences.

**Value Proposition:** Reduce churn by 15% through personalized experiences. Increase customer satisfaction scores by 20%. Reduce customer service costs by 10% through improved self-service.

**Pitch Angle:** Empower Claro to deliver exceptional, personalized customer experiences across all channels, driving loyalty and reducing churn.

**Objection Handling:**
- Objection: High initial investment. Response: Demonstrate long-term ROI through reduced churn, increased efficiency, and improved customer lifetime value.
- Objection: Integration complexity. Response: Offer phased implementation approach and provide dedicated support team.

**Success Metrics:**
- Customer satisfaction scores (CSAT)
- Net Promoter Score (NPS)
- Churn rate
- Customer lifetime value (LTV)
- Self-service adoption rate
- Customer service cost per interaction

**Implementation Approach:** Start with a pilot project focusing on a specific customer segment or service (e.g., new fiber optic customers). Integrate existing data sources and channels gradually.
