- **Factor:** 5G Spectrum Award
- **Description:** Paraguay's plan to award the 5G spectrum presents a regulatory milestone for Claro.
- **Details:** The 5G spectrum award in August 2025 may affect Claro's licensing and compliance requirements.

- **Factor:** 4G License Renewal
- **Description:** Claro and Tigo were granted 4G license renewal in Paraguay.
- **Details:** Renewal secures their market position but also aligns them with necessary regulatory checks.
- **Source:** Riskify Risk Report
- **Date:** 2021-09-02
