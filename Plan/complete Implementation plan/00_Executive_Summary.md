# Executive Summary: Autonomous Company Research System

## 1. Vision

To build an **autonomous, self-correcting AI research team** capable of generating deep, human-quality reports on companies and sectors. Unlike simple "search wrappers," this system mimics the cognitive processes of a human analyst: gathering data, forming hypotheses, challenging them, and synthesizing insights.

## 2. Core Philosophy: "Dynamic & Self-Correcting"

The system is not a linear pipeline. It is a **cyclic graph** that can:

- **Backtrack**: If data is missing during analysis, it goes back to gathering.
- **Debate**: A "Logic Critic" agent challenges conclusions to prevent hallucinations.
- **Remember**: A "Vault" stores knowledge across projects, allowing the system to get smarter over time.

## 3. Key Capabilities

- **Deep Due Diligence**: Financials, Market Size, Competitors, Risks (300+ data points).
- **Sector Intelligence**: Aggregates data from multiple companies to spot industry-wide trends.
- **B2B Sales Matching**: Matches a company's needs with a specific product portfolio.
- **Collaborative Mode**: Allows human analysts to intervene, approve, and guide the AI.

## 4. The "Newsroom" Model

We structure the agents like a digital newsroom:

- **Orchestrator (Editor-in-Chief)**: Assigns tasks and manages flow.
- **Specialists (Reporters)**: Dig for specific data (Financial, Market, Tech).
- **Analyst (Op-Ed)**: Synthesizes facts into strategy.
- **Critic (Fact-Checker)**: Reviews work for errors and gaps.

## 5. Strategic Value

- **Speed**: Reduces research time from days to minutes.
- **Depth**: Uncovers insights missed by quick searches.
- **Consistency**: Standardized output format for easy comparison.
- **Memory**: Builds a proprietary knowledge graph of the market.
