# Research Data: Online Sources (Primary Research)

**Purpose**: Data points that can be directly scraped, extracted, or accessed from public online sources.

**Sources**: Company websites, social media, job boards, news sites, public databases, APIs, review sites, etc.

---

## 🌐 Company Website & Public Pages

- Company Name
- Industry
- Headquarters Location
- Founded Date
- Company Description
- Core Products/Services (from website)
- Product Roadmap (if public)
- Recent Product Launches
- Pricing Information (if public)
- Contact Information
- Leadership Team Names & Titles
- Board Members (if listed)
- Press Releases
- Blog Posts
- Case Studies
- Customer Testimonials

## 💼 Job Boards (LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor)

- Total Job Postings
- Job Posts by Department (Engineering, Sales, Marketing)
- Job Titles (AI Specialist, Prompt Engineer, Data Entry Clerk, etc.)
- Job Descriptions (Keywords: "Digital Transformation", "Legacy System", "Manual Process")
- Hiring Velocity (New posts per month)
- Geographic Locations Hiring
- Salary Ranges (if posted)
- Required Skills
- Glassdoor Company Rating
- Glassdoor Employee Reviews
- Glassdoor CEO Approval Rating

## 📰 News & Media

- Press Mentions (Volume, Trend)
- News Article Titles & Summaries
- Publication Dates
- Media Outlets Covering Company
- PR Announcements
- Crisis Events (Negative news)
- Awards & Recognition
- Conference Speaking Engagements
- Podcast Appearances
- Webinar Hosting

- Follower Count

- Follower Count
- Post Frequency
- Engagement Rate
- Comment Volume

### YouTube

- Subscriber Count
- Video Upload Frequency
- View Counts
- Comment Volume

## 💰 Financial Data (Public Companies)

- SEC Filings

- Lead Investors
