# B2B Sales Strategy: Social Media Intelligence Suite

**Product**: Customer Feedback Analysis & Social Media Intelligence

**Objective**: Identify companies drowning in customer feedback (social media comments, internal surveys, questionnaires, feedback forms) but lacking the tools to extract actionable insights.

**The Philosophy**: We only reach out when we have evidence of a "feedback gap" - high volume, low analysis.

---

## 🎯 The Pain Points We Solve

### 1. **Unanalyzed Social Media Comments**

- **Signal**: High post engagement (100+ comments per post) but no visible response strategy
- **Pain**: Customer questions go unanswered, complaints ignored, sentiment trends missed
- **Our Solution**: Automated comment extraction + sentiment analysis + weekly reports

### 2. **Internal Feedback Chaos**

- **Signal**: They run employee surveys, customer questionnaires, NPS campaigns
- **Pain**: Data sits in spreadsheets, no one has time to analyze it, insights are lost
- **Our Solution**: Unified feedback dashboard with AI-powered theme extraction

### 3. **Manual Audit Processes**

- **Signal**: Quality assurance teams manually reviewing customer interactions
- **Pain**: Slow, expensive, inconsistent, can't scale
- **Our Solution**: Automated audit scoring + compliance tracking + trend detection

## 🎯 The Sales Playbook

### Step 1: Identify the Pain (Research Phase)

**Pain Index Scoring (0-100)**:

- **Comment Volume** (0-30 points): >1000/month = 30, 500-1000 = 20, 100-500 = 10
- **Response Gap** (0-30 points): No responses = 30, Slow responses = 20, Some responses = 10
- **Negative Sentiment** (0-20 points): Crisis event = 20, High negative % = 15, Moderate = 10
- **Internal Feedback** (0-20 points): Runs surveys but no action = 20, Has surveys = 10

**Action Thresholds**:

- **High Pain (70-100)**: Immediate outreach, high urgency
- **Medium Pain (40-69)**: Nurture campaign, educate on ROI
- **Low Pain (0-39)**: Long-term pipeline, monitor for triggers

### Step 2: Craft the Pitch (Personalization Phase)

**Email Template**:

```text
Subject: [Company Name] - 500+ unanswered comments on your last 10 posts

Hi [First Name],

I was analyzing [Company Name]'s social media presence and noticed something interesting:

- Your last 10 posts generated 500+ comments
- 73% of customer questions went unanswered
- Negative sentiment spiked 40% after [specific event]

This is a goldmine of customer insights that's currently being ignored.

We built a system that automatically:
✓ Extracts all comments from your social platforms
✓ Analyzes sentiment and identifies key themes
✓ Generates weekly reports with actionable insights

Would you be open to a 15-minute demo?

[Your Name]
```

### Step 3: Timing the Outreach (Trigger-Based)

Wait for a **trigger event**:

- **Negative PR Event** → "I saw the recent backlash on Twitter..."
- **Product Launch** → "Congrats on the launch! Are you tracking customer feedback?"
- **New CMO** → "Welcome to [Company]! I'd love to show you how we help with CX..."
- **Competitor Crisis** → "I noticed [Competitor] is struggling with reviews. Here's how to avoid that..."
