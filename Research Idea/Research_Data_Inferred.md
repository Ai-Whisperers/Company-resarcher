# Research Data: Inferred & Generated (Secondary Analysis)

**Purpose**: Data points that CANNOT be found directly online and must be inferred, calculated, or generated from the primary research data using AI analysis, pattern detection, and cross-referencing.

**Method**: AI-powered analysis of the data collected in `Research_Data_Online.md`.

---

## 💰 Financial Health (Inferred)

### From Job Posts + Funding Data

- **Burn Rate (Estimated)**: Calculate from headcount growth + average salaries + funding rounds
- **Runway (Months)**: Last funding amount ÷ estimated burn rate
- **Unit Economics (Estimated)**: Infer CAC from marketing spend signals, LTV from customer retention patterns

### From News + Social Media

- **Revenue Growth Trend**: Infer from "record quarter" announcements, expansion news
- **Profitability Status**: Infer from layoff news (unprofitable) vs. hiring spree (profitable)
- **Cash Flow Health**: Infer from payment delays mentioned in reviews, vendor complaints

### From Website + Tech Stack

- **Tech Debt Level**: Infer from old tech stack, slow website, downtime mentions
- **Infrastructure Costs**: Estimate from cloud provider choice, traffic volume

## 🌍 Market Position (Inferred)

### From Social Media + Reviews

- **Market Share (Estimated)**: Compare social media followers, review volume vs. competitors
- **Share of Voice**: Calculate from press mentions volume vs. competitors
- **Customer Sentiment Gap**: Compare your sentiment vs. top 3 competitors

### From Job Posts + News

- **Growth Stage Assessment**: Infer from hiring velocity, funding rounds, office expansions
- **Market Maturity**: Infer from competitor count, regulatory mentions, consolidation news

### From Product Pages + Reviews

- **Competitive Advantages (Moat)**: Identify from unique features, patents, customer lock-in signals
- **Threat of New Entrants**: Assess from barrier to entry (tech complexity, regulatory requirements)

## 🛠️ Product & Technology (Inferred)

### From Reviews + Social Media

- **Product-Market Fit Signals**: High NPS, low churn mentions, "can't live without it" reviews
- **Feature Gaps**: Identify from customer feature requests in comments, competitor comparisons

### From GitHub + Job Posts

- **Tech Stack Modernity**: Score based on technologies used (modern vs. legacy)

### From Patents + Publications

- **R&D Focus Areas**: Identify themes from patent titles, paper topics

## 👥 People & Culture (Inferred)

### From LinkedIn + Job Posts

- **Turnover Rate (Estimated)**: Track employee tenure changes, "former employee" updates
- **Hiring Velocity**: Calculate net new hires per month from LinkedIn headcount changes
- **Technical Talent Density**: % of engineering roles vs. total headcount
- **Talent Migration**: Identify key hires from competitors (e.g., "Ex-Google AI Lead joins Company X")

### From Glassdoor + Social Media

- **Culture Health Score**: Combine Glassdoor rating + employee social media sentiment
- **Leadership Stability**: Track C-suite changes, board member departures
- **Employee Morale**: Infer from review themes ("great culture" vs. "toxic environment")

### From Compensation Data + Funding

- **Equity Compensation Attractiveness**: Compare to market standards, funding stage
- **Founder Ownership (Estimated)**: Infer from funding dilution, cap table leaks

## 🔴 B2B Sales Signals (Inferred)

### From Social Media + Reviews

- **Pain Index Score (0-100)**: Calculate from:
  - Comment Volume (30 points)
  - Response Gap (30 points)
  - Negative Sentiment (20 points)
  - Internal Feedback Signals (20 points)

### From Job Posts + Tech Stack

- **AI Readiness Score**: Assess from:
  - AI job posts (hiring for AI roles)
  - Tech stack modernity
  - Innovation lab mentions
  - Executive AI strategy statements

### From Hiring + News

- **Manual Work Burden**: Identify from:
  - Data entry clerk job posts
  - "Manual process" complaints
  - Spreadsheet management mentions
  - Slow decision-making signals

### From Funding + Budget Signals

- **Budget Availability Score**: Calculate from:
  - Recent fundraising (fresh capital)
  - Profitable status (can afford tools)
  - Tech stack investments
  - Vendor spending patterns

### From LinkedIn + Conferences

- **Decision-Maker Accessibility**: Score from:
  - C-Suite LinkedIn activity
  - Conference attendance
  - Podcast appearances
  - Email accessibility (not behind gatekeepers)

### From Timing Signals

- **Trigger Event Urgency**: Identify:
  - Fiscal year start (new budgets)
  - Leadership changes (new priorities)
  - Product launches (need support)
  - Negative PR (urgency to fix)
